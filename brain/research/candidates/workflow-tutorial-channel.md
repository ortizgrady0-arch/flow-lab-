## TL;DR

- Demand is real and still rising: n8n hit $40M ARR (Jul 2025), 10x revenue and 6x user growth in the year to Oct 2025, a $2.5B Series C, 203.9k GitHub stars (fetched 2026-09-10), and its own docs say most of its 200k+ community learned from free YouTube tutorials. Tech/software long-form RPM is $8-30 (aggregators, 2026), the second-highest tier on YouTube.
- The format is on the right side of policy: real screen recordings with a human demonstrating a working automation are "production assistance", not "altered or synthetic content", and are the opposite of the templated mass-production the July 15 2025 inauthentic-content rule and the Jan 2026 sweep (16 channels, 35M subs, 4.7B views, secondary sources) targeted. Policy risk is low; the two open risks are YPP thresholds reportedly doubling to 8,000 hrs on Feb 1 2027 and affiliate programs cutting rates (Descript went from 15% recurring to a flat $25).
- Money comes in three layers with three clocks: affiliates first (n8n Cloud 30% recurring for 12 months, ElevenLabs 22% for 12 months, HeyGen 20-35%, Descript $25 flat; first dollar ~6-10 weeks), then a Gumroad template pack at $49-99 (net $42-85 after 10% + $0.50 + Stripe; multi-product sellers average 5.7x single-product sellers per a 146K-product scrape), then AdSense only after 1,000 subs + 4,000 hrs (month 8-14 at 10-15 hrs/week).
- Generic "n8n tutorial" is saturated and free-flooded: Nate Herk alone has 950K+ subscribers and a 450K free community; n8n's official library has 11,700+ free templates and two GitHub repos give away 4,343 (56.6k stars) and 280+ (25.3k stars) workflows. The only defensible slot is one profession the operator can credibly demo (bookkeepers, real-estate teams, photographers): scattered single videos exist, but no dedicated profession-specific channel was found this session.
- Automation is ~45% of weekly hours (time-weighted): Claude drafts the n8n JSON, script, metadata, Shorts cutdowns and comment replies; the human still has to build/test the automation and record the demo, which is exactly the part that keeps the channel monetizable.
- Economics are the weak point: startup ~$160, tools ~$70/mo, month-3 revenue $0-300, month-6 conservative $60 / base ~$450 / upside ~$2,500 at 10-12 hrs/week, i.e. roughly $1-50/hr with a base case near $9/hr. This is a strategic distribution asset for [[research/shortlist]] picks 5-7 and the template product, not a fast earner.
- Legal footnotes that matter: the US Copyright Office (Jan 29 2025) says purely prompt-generated output is not copyrightable, so template packs are protected mainly by license terms and the accompanying human-written docs/video, not copyright; n8n's Sustainable Use License forbids commercial redistribution of the software but is silent on selling workflows/courses (n8n runs its own affiliate and creator programs, so the practice is tolerated); FTC affiliate disclosures must be verbal in-video and in the first lines of the description (penalties up to $53,088 per violation).
- Verdict: go, but as the channel that feeds the profession-vertical products; pick the profession by the operator's ability to show a real end-to-end workflow, publish weekly for 26 weeks before judging, and launch the template pack by video 10-12 rather than waiting for YPP.

## 4. Automation map

Weekly cycle for one 12-25 minute tutorial, two to three Shorts, and amortized product work. Levels: full = runs without the operator; assisted = AI drafts, human edits/verifies; manual = human does it. Costs are monthly for the tool that carries the step.

| # | Step | Level | Tool(s) (monthly cost) | Time/unit | Quality risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Acquire audience: topic mining (profession subreddits/forums, n8n changelog, own comments) ranked by pain and search intent | assisted | n8n RSS/Reddit nodes -> Claude ($20 Pro; n8n Cloud Starter ~$24 or self-host ~$6 VPS) | 0.5 h | Drifts to generic topics; must be filtered by "would a bookkeeper search this" |
| 2 | Build and test the actual automation | assisted | Claude (drafts n8n JSON, code nodes, prompts), n8n, the profession's SaaS trial accounts | 3.0 h | Highest risk: broken flows on camera, API/pricing changes, hallucinated node parameters; must be run end-to-end before recording |
| 3 | Script/outline with timestamps | assisted | Claude | 0.5 h | Over-polished "AI voice"; keep it an outline, talk naturally |
| 4 | Screen recording + own voice narration | manual | OBS (free) or Descript recorder; USB mic | 1.5 h | This is the authenticity layer; do not replace with AI voice (allowed, but weakens the policy position and trust) |
| 5 | Edit: cuts, filler-word removal, zooms, captions | assisted | Descript Creator ($24 annual / $35 monthly; Studio Sound, filler removal) | 1.5 h | Over-cut pacing; captions mis-transcribe node names |
| 6 | Title/thumbnail/description/chapters/tags | assisted | Claude + Canva free | 0.5 h | Clickbait drift; keep profession terms in title |
| 7 | Shorts cutdowns (2-3) as funnel | assisted | Descript clips or free CapCut | 0.5 h | Shorts do not count toward 4,000 hrs; keep to 20% of effort |
| 8 | Publish/schedule, insert affiliate links + FTC disclosure, cross-post | full | n8n (YouTube node, Google API), template description | 0.25 h | Disclosure text must be in first three lines; verify link tracking |
| 9 | Comment replies and community posts | assisted | Claude drafts from comment export via n8n, human approves | 0.5 h | Generic replies read as bot; answer technical questions yourself |
| 10 | Productize: package workflows, README, install video, changelog | assisted | Claude, Descript | 1.0 h (amortized) | Templates break on n8n version bumps; needs a maintenance promise |
| 11 | Listing, delivery, buyer onboarding email | full | Gumroad (0 fixed; 10% + $0.50 + Stripe), n8n webhook -> email | 0.25 h | Gumroad Discover sales cost 30% |
| 12 | Customer support and refunds | manual | Email | 0.5 h | Non-technical buyers need install help; bake into a paid install tier instead of free support |
| 13 | Affiliate program admin and link tracking | manual | Program dashboards, a Sheet | 0.25 h | Rate changes (Descript) and 90-day holds (ElevenLabs) |
| 14 | Getting paid | full | Gumroad (MoR), AdSense, affiliate networks | 0 | $100 Gumroad minimum payout; AdSense $100 threshold |

Time-weighted automation: total ≈ 10.75 h/week; automated-equivalent hours (full = 1.0, assisted = 0.5, manual = 0) ≈ 4.75 h. **Overall automation ≈ 44% (call it 45%).** The product side alone (steps 10-11, 14) is ~60% automated; the channel side is ~40%. Full automation is structurally capped because steps 2 and 4 are the monetization licence.

## 6. Platform, policy and legal risks

- **YouTube inauthentic-content policy (effective 2025-07-15).** YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" and clarified it covers mass-produced and repetitive/templated videos; YouTube called it a clarification of an existing rule, said reaction/clip content is unaffected, and warned that non-compliant channels can be suspended or removed from YPP. [S: Social Media Today; Search Engine Journal; support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392; TubeBuddy; Gulf News, July 2025] Screen-recorded, human-built, human-narrated tutorials are the named counter-example; risk is low as long as each video is a distinct workflow, not a re-skinned template.
- **2026 enforcement wave.** Secondary sources report 16 channels terminated in January 2026 (35M combined subscribers, 4.7B lifetime views, ~$10M/yr ad revenue) for mass-produced content, with YouTube stressing the rule targets templated scale, not AI tools; a July 15 2026 TechTimes piece ("now it's judging your taste") suggests further quality signals in recommendations. [S: ytgrowth.io; lenspov; outlierkit; bottlerocketcontent; techtimes 2026-07-15] Not verified from a YouTube primary source.
- **AI disclosure ("altered or synthetic content").** Disclosure is required only for realistic content a viewer could mistake for real footage of a person/place/event; not required for production assistance, scripts, clearly unrealistic content, or plain AI voiceover (unless cloning a real person). Voluntary labels do not affect reach or monetization per YouTube. [S: blog.youtube disclosing-ai-generated-content; support.google.com/youtube/answer/15447836; shortsfast, ytzolo 2026] A screen recording of n8n with the operator's voice triggers nothing.
- **YPP thresholds.** Current: 1,000 subs + 4,000 qualified public watch hours (12 months) or 10M qualified Shorts views (90 days); early-access tier at 500 subs. Multiple 2026 secondary sources now state that from February 1 2027 the requirement rises to 8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views with 1,000 subs unchanged. [S: AIR Media-Tech; vidIQ YPP guide; LensPOV; tubemilestone 2026] Still not read from YouTube Help directly; treat as likely. Implication: a channel started in Sep 2026 should aim to apply before Feb 2027 or plan for the doubled bar.
- **FTC Endorsement Guides.** Any affiliate link is a material connection: disclose in plain language where the endorsement appears, verbally in the video near the recommendation and in writing in the first three lines of the description; "#ad" or "I earn a commission" language; civil penalties up to $53,088 per violation (2025 figure, inflation-adjusted). [S: ftc.gov Endorsement Guides FAQ; referralcandy; promise.legal; ytcalculators 2026] Build the disclosure into the publishing template (step 8).
- **Copyright status of AI output.** US Copyright Office Part 2 report (2025-01-29): purely prompt-generated material is not copyrightable; human contributions are assessed case by case; AI used for ideation/outline does not taint the human work. [S: copyright.gov Part 2 report; Jones Day, Skadden, Crowell client alerts, Feb 2025] Practical effect: a Claude-generated n8n JSON has weak or no copyright; protect the pack with a written license, human-authored documentation and video, and by selling the install/maintenance rather than the file. Expect copies.
- **n8n Sustainable Use License.** Permits use for internal business, personal or non-commercial purposes and free non-commercial distribution; prohibits distributing the software for commercial gain; silent on selling workflows, courses or consulting. [V: github.com/n8n-io/n8n LICENSE.md] Because n8n operates a public affiliate program and template/creator program, selling workflow JSON and teaching n8n is standard practice, but do not bundle or resell n8n itself, and do not white-label hosted n8n for buyers without checking the license FAQ (docs page could not be fetched: no data found).
- **Affiliate program TOS.** Typical restrictions (self-referrals, coupon sites, brand-bid paid ads) apply; Descript's cut to $25 flat and ElevenLabs' 90-day hold show commission terms can change without notice. [S] n8n affiliate cookie window and payout terms: no data found.
- **Gumroad.** 10% + $0.50 direct, 30% on Discover, $100 minimum payout since March 2026, Gumroad is Merchant of Record (handles VAT/sales tax). [S] Gumroad rules on AI-generated goods: no data found this session.
- **Tool TOS (Claude/Anthropic).** Not researched this session (budget); no data found. Prior vault notes assume normal commercial use of Claude output is permitted for a paying account; re-verify the Usage Policy before selling packs built with Claude.
- **Profession-specific liability.** Tutorials touching bookkeeping or real-estate compliance should carry a "not accounting/legal advice" note; an automation that mis-files a tax document is the buyer's risk, but the template license should say so.

## 7. Skills needed and learning curve (for the stated operator profile)

- **n8n fluency (have: comfortable with no-code automation).** Needs to reach "debug someone else's broken flow" level: expressions, error handling, credentials, webhooks, sub-workflows, version upgrades. Roughly 20-40 hours of deliberate practice on top of the profile's existing comfort [E]; Nate Herk's free 8-hour course is the standard on-ramp. [S: classcentral]
- **Profession domain knowledge (gap).** The credential is the demo, but the demo must use the profession's real tools (QuickBooks Online, Dext, Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Pixieset, HoneyBook). Plan 2-4 weeks of trial accounts and 5-10 conversations with practitioners before video 1; pick the profession where the operator already has a contact or prior job exposure.
- **On-mic presentation (gap, moderate).** Screen-recorded narration is the easiest on-camera format; the first 5-8 videos will be rough. Descript filler-word removal covers a lot. No face needed.
- **Editing (gap, low).** Descript/OBS are learnable in a weekend; the lens notes and the design of this candidate assume no motion graphics.
- **Light scripting (willing to learn).** JavaScript in n8n Code nodes and basic API/JSON literacy are required to demo credibly; Claude Code covers most drafting.
- **Packaging and support (gap, low-moderate).** Writing a README a bookkeeper can follow, versioning templates, handling refunds.
- **Compliance habits (gap, low).** FTC disclosure boilerplate, not cloning voices, one distinct workflow per video.
- **Not needed:** a professional credential, a face, paid ads, a community platform until 20+ buyers.

## TL;DR

- Every major marketplace now *permits* AI-made digital goods but gates them behind disclosure: Etsy Creativity Standards (AI listings must be tagged and attributed "Designed by", not "Made by"), KDP (AI-generated vs AI-assisted checkbox since Sept 2023, plus a 3-new-titles/day cap), DistroKid/Spotify (AI Credits beta from 2026-04-16), Adobe Stock (labeled gen-AI accepted). Non-disclosure, not AI use, is what gets shops removed.
- Median outcomes are small and heavily skewed: Gumroad median creator ~$72/mo with 99.5% of revenue to the top 1% (third-party scrape of 146K products); Etsy digital-download median roughly £400-800/mo per aggregator blogs (anecdotal); PromptBase ~20 of 1,000 prompts ever sell. Plan for 6-12 months to first $100 with no audience (Gumroad data).
- Best-evidenced 2026-current plays for a no-audience solo operator: (1) micro-profession Notion+Sheets operating templates, (2) boring local-service Canva form templates on Etsy, (3) script-generated large-print puzzle books on KDP (coloring is the most saturated low-content corner), (4) one-feature marketplace-compliance micro-SaaS built with Claude Code (a visible cluster of 2026 competitors proves demand).
- Amazon Merch on Demand restructured royalties on 2026-06-01: a $19.99 tee pays $2.44 at Creator tier vs $4.88 at Plus tier (needs 15%+ external traffic), so Merch is now a Pinterest/social-traffic game; approvals ~30-40%.
- AI music is the highest policy-risk item: UMG settled with Udio (Oct 2025), WMG with both (Nov 2025), but Sony v. Suno/Udio and UMG v. Suno are still live in 2026. Suno commercial rights only on Pro ($10) / Premier ($30) and not retroactive for free-tier songs.
- Udemy economics deteriorated (subscription pool 25% in 2023 to 15% by Jan 2026; instructors keep 37% of organic marketplace sales; GenAI program auto-enrolls content) so courses should be sold direct (Gumroad/Skool) and only make sense paired with a YouTube/social channel.
- Flag as saturated or ceiling-limited: prompt packs, GPT Store builder payouts ($100-500/mo soft ceiling), generic planners/wall-art quotes/wedding printables on Etsy (~4.5M digital listings), KDP coloring books, Adobe Stock AI imagery ($0.25-0.50/download).
- Research gaps this session (web access cut off after 20 searches): laser-cut SVG demand, Google Sheets template counts, Creative Fabrica AI policy, Shutterstock/Freepik AI acceptance, YouTube July-2025 "inauthentic content" wording, Whop stats. All marked "no data found" below.

## Method and evidence quality

20 WebSearch queries run 2026-09-10; all WebFetch attempts (etsy.com, kdp.amazon.com, sec.gov, canva.com, support.udemy.com, support.google.com, plus ~30 secondary blogs) were blocked by the network egress proxy, so official policy text is quoted *via* the search-result summaries of secondary guides, not from the primary page. Labels used below:

- **Verified** = platform filing, pricing page, or help-center content surfaced in results (e.g. Etsy Q2 2026 10-Q figures, Canva Creators help page, Suno plan prices as quoted by multiple pricing guides).
- **Anecdotal** = income claims from blogs, gurus, Reddit, or third-party scrapes with unpublished methodology.
- **No data found** = could not verify this session.

Platform baseline (verified): Etsy Q2 2026 marketplace GMS $2.6B (+7.5% YoY), 5.706M active sellers (+5.9% YoY), ~87M active buyers, revenue $668M, trailing-12-month GMS per buyer $124 (Etsy Q2 2026 10-Q via Yahoo Finance / Digital Commerce 360, Aug 2026).

## Candidate 1: Large-print themed puzzle books on KDP (script-generated)

See [[research/candidates/kdp-large-print-puzzle-books]].

**Offer.** Paperback puzzle books (word search, sudoku, mazes, number-fill) for a tight audience: e.g. "Large Print Word Search for Nurses", "Bird-Watcher's Word Search", "Sudoku for Seniors, Level 1-3". Puzzles are generated by a Python script (written with Claude), laid out with a script-driven PDF pipeline, cover built in Canva.

**Demand signals.**
- Coloring books are "the most saturated corner of low-content KDP"; puzzle books "still sell steadily" and are called "a healthier bet" (KDP Easy FAQ, 2026; Midas Tools, 2026 — anecdotal guru sources).
- KDP requires AI disclosure at upload since Sept 2023 (after Authors Guild talks) and now caps publishers at 3 new titles/day (The Passive Voice, 2026; Univers Studio, 2026; Writer Cosmos, 2026). The cap is a signal that volume-spam is what Amazon is fighting, which favors small curated catalogs.
- Income evidence is thin: one creator reported ~$18K over four years from low-content books (Miller Bolo Substack, cited by search results, 2026 — anecdotal). Puzzle-specific 2026 sales data: **no data found** (search budget exhausted before the query ran).
- Copyright nuance: US Copyright Office treats purely AI-generated output as uncopyrightable; human selection/arrangement is what is protectable (The Passive Voice, 2026). Program-generated puzzles with human-chosen word lists sit closer to the protectable side, but KDP's AI-generated definition ("text, images, translations created by an AI-based tool") is ambiguous for code-generated puzzles; disclose conservatively.

**AI / automation breakdown (~80% automated).**
- Automated end-to-end: puzzle generation (Python + Claude-written generator, $0), interior PDF layout (ReportLab/LaTeX script), title/subtitle/keyword/description drafting (Claude Pro ~$20/mo), cover text variants.
- Human: niche selection and word-list curation (this is the differentiator and the copyright-relevant human input), cover art direction in Canva (free or Pro ~$15/mo), KDP upload and the AI-disclosure choice, checking a printed proof (~$5-8 per proof).
- Tools: Claude Pro ~$20/mo, Python (free), Canva free/Pro, KDP (free; Amazon takes print cost + 40% of list price on paperbacks — standard KDP terms, not re-verified this session).

**Numbers.** Startup ~$60-100 (2 months Claude Pro, 2-3 proof copies). First dollar: 2-6 weeks (KDP review is days; discovery is slow). Realistic month-6: $0-300/mo (anchored to the ~$375/mo four-year average above; most titles sell zero). Saturation: medium. Policy risk: medium (disclosure ambiguity for code-generated content; 3-title/day cap; quality/"low-content" enforcement).

## Candidate 2: Micro-profession "operating system" templates (Notion + companion Google Sheet)

See [[research/candidates/notion-sheets-micro-profession-templates]].

**Offer.** A Notion workspace plus a matching Google Sheet for one narrow occupation whose workflow you can fully specify: e.g. "Solo wedding photographer client pipeline + pricing calculator", "Etsy digital seller fee & profit tracker", "Mobile notary job log + mileage sheet". Sold on Notion Marketplace, Gumroad, and Etsy at $19-49, with a free lite version as the funnel.

**Demand signals.**
- Notion Marketplace takes 10% + $0.40/transaction (Kupkaike, 2026; Fungies, 2026). Reported creator earnings: "most active creators $500-3,000/mo", beginners "$500-1,500/mo within six months" (Coachli, Kupkaike, 2026 — anecdotal). Named outliers: Thomas Frank >$1M lifetime, Easlo six figures from one niche, Pascio >$180K from 50+ templates (widely reported, anecdotal).
- Success factor named across every 2026 guide: a specific niche plus a content channel (YouTube/TikTok/newsletter) plus multiple price points (Kupkaike, Coachli, Easy.tools, 2026). This matches the operator's YouTube interest.
- Gumroad scrape: multi-product sellers (3+) average $5,201 vs single-product sellers, 5.7x more; time to first $100 is 60-90 days with an audience and 6-12 months without (Insight Raider "State of Gumroad 2026", third-party scrape of 146,271 products, $206M tracked).
- Google Sheets template demand on Etsy specifically: **no data found** (query blocked).

**AI / automation breakdown (~55% automated).**
- Automated: workflow research and interview synthesis (Claude), database schema, formulas, property names, help text, listing copy, SEO tags, FAQ, email sequence, and n8n/Make automation that delivers the Notion duplicate link on purchase (n8n cloud ~$24/mo or self-host free; Make free tier).
- Human: choosing the profession, validating with 3-5 real practitioners, the Notion build itself (Notion has no reliable API for template creation, so it is drag-and-drop), screenshots/mockups, a 3-5 minute demo video (this is where YouTube Shorts fit).
- Tools: Notion free, Claude Pro ~$20/mo, Canva free, Gumroad (10% + $0.50 + Stripe ~2.9% + $0.30), Etsy $0.20/listing (per PromptBase multi-platform guide, 2026), Notion Marketplace 10% + $0.40.

**Numbers.** Startup ~$40-80. First dollar: 4-10 weeks. Realistic month-6 with no audience: $50-400/mo (Gumroad no-audience timeline dominates; the "$500-1,500 in six months" claims assume a content channel). Saturation: medium (generic "second brain"/planner templates are saturated; micro-profession angle is not). Policy risk: low (no AI-disclosure requirement on Notion Marketplace found; Etsy listing must be tagged AI and "Designed by").

## Candidate 3: Editable Canva business-form templates for boring local-service niches (Etsy)

See [[research/candidates/etsy-canva-local-service-forms]].

**Offer.** Bundles of editable Canva templates that a one-person local business actually needs: dog-groomer intake + vaccine record forms, lash-tech consent forms, mobile-detailing price sheets, house-cleaner checklists. Listed on Etsy at $8-25 per bundle, delivered as a Canva share link PDF. Optionally cross-listed on Gumroad.

**Demand signals.**
- Etsy digital-download category has ~4.5M listings in 2026 and the top sub-categories (planners, wall-art quotes, wedding printables) are saturated (SideQuestHustle / ListifyAI, 2026 — anecdotal aggregator). B2B micro-niche forms avoid those.
- Income aggregators (anecdotal): median digital-download seller £400-800/mo, "roughly double the all-category median"; beginners $10-100/week; top 10% £8K-30K+/mo from 50-200 listings (ListifyAI, RichTactic, 2026). Craftybase profitability post: average digital shop $29,324/yr revenue, $5,865/yr profit (2026, methodology unclear).
- Etsy policy (secondary quotes of Creativity Standards, updated 2024 and "reinforced in 2026"): AI-made digital products allowed if you supplied creative direction; the listing must be marked AI and attributed "Designed by [seller]" not "Made by"; violations lead to listing removal and, for repeat/large-scale, shop suspension (Bulk Mockup, Elistit, Artomate, Inkfluence AI, 2026).
- Etsy marketplace health is verified: GMS $2.6B in Q2 2026, +7.5% YoY, 87M buyers (Etsy 10-Q via Yahoo Finance, Aug 2026).

**AI / automation breakdown (~50% automated).**
- Automated: niche discovery (Claude drafts a list of 30 local-service occupations and their paperwork), form copy and legal-ish boilerplate (with a disclaimer; not legal advice), listing titles/tags/descriptions, mockup text, buyer-message autoresponders, bulk listing via CSV.
- Human: the Canva layout work (Canva's Bulk Create and Brand Kit speed it up but a person still designs), mockup images, one round of feedback from a real practitioner, Etsy shop setup and the AI-disclosure/attribution fields on each listing.
- Tools: Canva Pro ~$15/mo, Claude Pro ~$20/mo, Etsy $0.20/listing (other Etsy fee components not re-verified this session), eRank/Alura free tiers for keyword checks (not verified this session).

**Numbers.** Startup ~$60-100 (Canva Pro x3 months + ~50 listings). First dollar: 2-6 weeks (Etsy digital shops commonly see first sales within a month per the aggregators; anecdotal). Realistic month-6: $100-500/mo. Saturation: high for the category, medium for the B2B-form angle. Policy risk: medium (disclosure and attribution must be correct on every listing; Etsy enforcement escalated in 2026 per multiple guides).

## Candidate 4: One-feature marketplace-compliance micro-SaaS built with Claude Code

See [[research/candidates/marketplace-compliance-micro-saas]].

**Offer.** A single-purpose web tool that answers one 2026 compliance question for a seller: "Is this Etsy listing correctly disclosed under the Creativity Standards?" (paste listing, get a checklist and a rewritten description), or "Which KDP AI checkbox applies to this manuscript?", or "Am I on track for Merch on Demand Plus tier (15%+ external traffic) this month?". Freemium; $7-15/mo or a $29 one-time.

**Demand signals.**
- A visible cluster of 2026 competitors already sells exactly this: iscompliant.app, artomate.app, elistit.com, inkfluenceai.com, ngini.com, rewarx.com, bulkmockup.com all published Etsy-AI-disclosure content in 2026 (search results, 2026). Multiple funded/indie entrants is the strongest demand signal in this lens; it also means the "Etsy" variant is the most crowded of the three.
- Policy churn that creates the need is verified: Etsy AI tagging + "Designed by" attribution (2024, reinforced 2026); KDP AI disclosure + 3 titles/day (Sept 2023 / 2026); Amazon Merch 3-tier royalties from 2026-06-01 with Plus tier requiring 15%+ external traffic (Merch Titans, AMZ Prep, 2026).
- Build-cost collapse: 2026 guides claim a simple SaaS with auth, payments, dashboard, and landing page now takes "2 to 4 days" with Claude Code/Cursor vs 2-4 weeks before (AI Magicx, 2026 — anecdotal). Claim that "34% of new micro-SaaS products launched in Q1 2026 were built by founders with no prior programming experience" (LOOTR, 2026 — unverified, source not given). Outlier anecdotes: a solo dev with 35 micro-SaaS apps at $77K in one month (BuildMVPFast, 2026 — unverified), Pieter Levels' fly.pieter.com $1M ARR in 17 days (widely reported, 2025).
- Revenue for compliance-tool micro-SaaS specifically: **no data found**.

**AI / automation breakdown (~70% automated at build, ~40% in operation).**
- Automated: the whole codebase (Claude Code on Claude Pro ~$20/mo or Max ~$100-200/mo), rule-engine prompts that classify listings/manuscripts, landing page copy, docs, SEO articles, Stripe checkout, email onboarding (n8n), changelog.
- Human: keeping the rule set current as marketplaces change (weekly policy reading), customer support, distribution (Reddit r/EtsySellers, r/selfpublish, Facebook groups, YouTube demos), and the judgment call of what "AI-generated" means for each edge case.
- Tools: Claude Pro/Max, Vercel free tier, Supabase free tier, Stripe (2.9% + $0.30), domain ~$12/yr, Resend/Postmark free tier, n8n self-hosted free.

**Numbers.** Startup ~$100-250 (Claude subscription 2-3 months + domain). First dollar: 3-8 weeks. Realistic month-6: $0-500 MRR (no benchmark for this niche; most micro-SaaS never pass $100 MRR — no 2026 source found for that base rate). Saturation: medium (Etsy variant high; KDP/Merch variants lower). Policy risk: low for the tool itself, but the value proposition depends on marketplaces continuing to change rules, and Etsy/Amazon could ship the same check natively.

## Candidate 5: Labeled generative-AI stock image portfolios on Adobe Stock

See [[research/candidates/adobe-stock-ai-portfolio]].

**Offer.** 500-2,000 Firefly-generated, metadata-rich images in a conceptual niche that photographers under-serve (e.g. abstract fintech backgrounds, inclusive workplace illustrations, seasonal flat-lays), uploaded to Adobe Stock with the gen-AI label.

**Demand signals.**
- Adobe Stock explicitly accepts gen-AI content (Firefly, Midjourney, DALL-E named) if labeled and if the generator's license permits stock submission; royalties follow the same schedule as other content; a Firefly Contributor bonus paid for content considered for training (window June 3 2024 to June 2 2025) (Adobe Stock contributor help pages, surfaced 2026 — verified policy).
- Earnings per download: contributor reports "early 2026" put AI content at $0.25-0.50/download vs $0.33-1.00 for traditional (AI Metadata Cleaner blog, 2026 — anecdotal). Upload limits and review times for 2026: **no data found** (Medium post exists but was unreachable).
- Shutterstock/Freepik/Getty AI-contributor acceptance: **no data found** this session (query blocked). Do not assume Shutterstock accepts AI submissions.

**AI / automation breakdown (~85% automated).**
- Automated: prompt batches (Claude writes 200 prompt variants per theme), generation (Firefly via Adobe plan ~$10/mo, or Midjourney ~$10-30/mo), titles/keywords via vision model, CSV metadata export, upload batching.
- Human: curation and rejection of artifacts (hands, text, logos, real-people likeness), verifying no brand/artist-style violations, niche choice, monthly review of what sold.
- Tools: Adobe Firefly ~$10/mo, Claude Pro ~$20/mo, optional Midjourney ~$10/mo.

**Numbers.** Startup ~$30-60. First dollar: 4-12 weeks (review queues plus first license). Realistic month-6: $20-150/mo (at $0.25-0.50/download you need 100-300 downloads/mo). Saturation: high (AI imagery floods every stock site). Policy risk: medium (tool-license and likeness rules; per-download rates trending down).

## Candidate 6: Royalty-free AI background-music bundles for creators (Suno Premier) + DistroKid distribution

See [[research/candidates/suno-background-music-bundles]].

**Offer.** Themed instrumental packs ("30 lo-fi tracks for study streams", "cozy vlog beds", "podcast stingers") sold on Gumroad at $15-39 with a clear license, plus the same catalog distributed to Spotify/Apple as a "focus" artist via DistroKid with AI credits declared.

**Demand signals.**
- Suno plans: Pro $10/mo ($8 annual, 2,500 credits) and Premier $30/mo ($24 annual, 10,000 credits, Suno Studio); only Pro/Premier grant commercial rights and they are **not retroactive** for songs made on the free tier; one 2026 guide lists download caps of 20/mo (Pro) and 60/mo (Premier) (Dynamoi, Music Make AI, Lumi Music, Tech Jack Solutions, Margabagus — 2026 pricing guides; primary page unreachable).
- Distribution: DistroKid permits AI music with AI-credit disclosure (lyrics/vocals/instrumental/full composition); Spotify has no blanket ban and launched an AI Credits beta on 2026-04-16 starting with DistroKid; enforcement targets impersonation, spam, and artificial streaming (Soundverse, Jack Righteous, Reposter Network, RightsDocket, Undetectr — 2026).
- Legal overhang (verified via multiple trackers): UMG settled with Udio Oct 2025 (per-generation royalties reported at $0.002-0.005) and WMG settled with both Udio and Suno Nov 2025; Sony is still litigating both and UMG still sues Suno, which is arguing fair use citing Bartz; AFM sued UMG/WMG over the settlements (Music Business Worldwide, Hollywood Reporter, AI Lawsuit Tracker, Chartlex, Forbes 2025-12-18). The licensed Udio platform launching 2026 is a walled garden with a per-generation royalty model.
- YouTube's July 2025 "inauthentic/mass-produced content" monetization wording: **not re-verified this session** (support.google.com blocked). Treat AI-music YouTube channels as monetization-risky.
- Revenue for solo Suno sellers: **no data found**.

**AI / automation breakdown (~75% automated).**
- Automated: prompt/style generation (Claude), track generation (Suno), mastering pass (free tools or Suno Studio), cover art (Firefly), Gumroad listings, DistroKid metadata, n8n-driven license PDF on purchase.
- Human: listening QA (Suno artifacts, accidental melodic similarity), licence drafting, choosing pack themes from creator requests, and monitoring the lawsuits.
- Tools: Suno Premier $30/mo, DistroKid (~$23/yr, not verified this session), Claude Pro ~$20/mo, Gumroad fees.

**Numbers.** Startup ~$90-120. First dollar: 3-8 weeks. Realistic month-6: $0-200/mo. Saturation: high (Spotify and YouTube are flooded with AI lo-fi). Policy risk: **high** (unsettled litigation, non-retroactive commercial rights, download caps, platform disclosure regimes still moving).

## Candidate 7: Amazon Merch on Demand hobby-niche designs with Pinterest-driven external traffic

See [[research/candidates/merch-on-demand-external-traffic]].

**Offer.** 25-100 AI-assisted (human-directed) typography/illustration designs for one hobby micro-niche (e.g. pickleball club humor, beekeeping, disc golf), listed on Merch on Demand, with Pinterest pins and short-form video driving the external traffic needed for Plus tier.

**Demand signals.**
- From 2026-06-01 Amazon replaced flat royalties with three tiers: Creator (organic-only) pays $2.44 on a $19.99 tee; Plus (15%+ external traffic) pays $4.88; Premium is higher (Merch Titans, AMZ Prep, 2026 — industry blogs quoting Amazon's announcement). This doubles royalty for anyone who can send their own traffic, which is exactly the social-media skill the operator wants.
- AI rules: AI-assisted designs allowed if you hold the rights; fully AI designs with no human creative input risk rejection/account issues (Merch Titans, MyDesigns, 2026).
- Approval rate ~30-40% of applications in 2026 after a wave of AI spam accounts (Nexscope, 2026 — anecdotal).
- Per-seller income data for 2026: **no data found**.

**AI / automation breakdown (~65% automated).**
- Automated: niche phrase mining (Claude), design generation (Firefly/Ideogram-class tools for text-in-image, ~$10-20/mo), background removal, listing bullets, Pinterest pin images and captions batched via Canva + n8n, weekly analytics summary.
- Human: application essay, brand/trademark checks per design, final creative selection, posting cadence on Pinterest/TikTok, responding to rejections.
- Tools: Claude Pro ~$20/mo, Firefly or Ideogram ~$10-20/mo, Canva free, Pinterest free, n8n self-hosted free.

**Numbers.** Startup ~$0-40. First dollar: 4-12 weeks (approval wait plus tier-10 cap on live designs). Realistic month-6: $0-150/mo. Saturation: high. Policy risk: medium (approval lottery, tier system, trademark strikes).

## Saturated or restricted in this lens

- **Prompt packs / PromptBase.** 80/20 split to seller, ~$3M monthly marketplace volume claimed (unverified), but "out of every 1,000 prompts published, maybe 20 ever make a sale and 5 break $500/month"; beginners $50-200/mo after three months (Grey Journal, AI Profit Mode, NeuraPulse — 2026, anecdotal). Ceiling too low for the time invested; Etsy prompt-pack listings also require AI disclosure.
- **Custom GPTs / GPT Store payouts.** Builder payouts are an engagement-weighted pool with a "soft ceiling of $100-500/month unless you reach the top 0.01%", ID-verified builders only; OpenAI opened a ChatGPT App Directory for third-party apps, and Claude Skills are free with no direct payout (Wildnet Edge, Digital Applied, VentureBeat, 2026). Useful as a lead magnet, not as income.
- **KDP coloring books.** "Most saturated corner of low-content KDP" (KDP Easy, Midas Tools 2026); 3-titles/day cap; AI images must be disclosed as AI-generated. Prefer puzzle books (Candidate 1).
- **Generic Etsy printables** (planners, wall-art quotes, wedding printables) inside a ~4.5M-listing category (anecdotal count); Etsy escalates enforcement on undisclosed AI in 2026.
- **Udemy as a channel.** 37% organic share, 15% subscription pool, GenAI auto-enrolment (see Candidate 8).
- **Adobe Stock AI imagery** is allowed but pays $0.25-0.50/download (anecdotal); volume play only.
- **AI music on streaming** is legally allowed with disclosure but the Sony/UMG cases against Suno remain open in 2026; treat as speculative (Candidate 6).
- **Amazon Merch at Creator tier** pays $2.44 per $19.99 tee from 2026-06-01; without external traffic it is not worth the design time.
- **Not researched (web access cut off):** laser-cut/Cricut SVG bundles, Creative Fabrica AI policy and payouts, Whop marketplace stats, Shutterstock/Freepik AI acceptance, Google Sheets template demand, Canva Creators template payout rates (program is in beta, application + portfolio required, royalty pool with no published per-use rate, $10/$20 payout thresholds per Canva help pages).

## TL;DR
- Strongest evidenced *services* in this lens: (1) QC'd multi-language audio tracks for YouTube educators (YouTube reports 6M+ daily viewers of auto-dubbed content and 25%+ non-primary-language watch time on custom tracks; ElevenLabs dubbing ~$0.33-0.50/min), (2) inbound AI missed-call receptionist for plumbing/HVAC (Vapi $0.05/min, Retell $0.07/min base; resale $200-800/mo), (3) AI UGC ad-variation packs for small DTC brands (Arcads ~$11/video, Creatify $39-99/mo).
- Strongest evidenced *products*: personalized AI-illustrated kids books on Etsy ($2.50 digital to $40-55 printed, active market pages) and vertical n8n workflow templates ($29-299 each; marketplaces pay 85% to seller; Upwork n8n rates $40-100/hr).
- Vibe-coded micro-tools are real but high-variance; sell on the web via Stripe, not the App Store (Apple is reportedly rejecting AI-built spam apps). Named-founder revenue evidence exists (Forbes, Mar 2026) but most "$X MRR in 4 months" stories are unverified marketing.
- AI evaluation gigs (Mercor $25-250/hr, Outlier $15-80/hr, DataAnnotation $15-45/hr) are real cash flow but ~0% automatable and work availability is inconsistent; treat as a bridge, not a hustle.
- Saturated/restricted: raw AI music on streaming (Deezer: >50% of daily uploads AI by Jul 2026, 85% of AI-music streams flagged fraudulent and demonetized; Spotify spam filter since Sep 2025), AI beat-compilation YouTube channels (inauthentic-content policy Jul 2025; 11 channels/35M subs terminated by Jan 2026), fully AI podcasts (35.4% of new Spotify feeds already AI, May 2026), GPT Store payouts (still invite-only pilot; $100-500/mo ceiling anecdotal), Claude "skills" marketplaces (third-party, $4-15/skill, no demand data), Anthropic Marketplace (enterprise-only).
- Biggest structural risk across the lens: platforms bundling the AI feature for free (YouTube auto-dubbing with Expressive Speech and lip-sync pilot; Jobber/ServiceTitan native AI receptionists at $29-99). The durable margin is in QC, integration, and vertical specificity, not in pressing the generate button.
- Research constraint: 14 web searches completed before the session-wide search budget (200/200) was exhausted; every WebFetch of a primary page was egress-blocked. All figures below are from search-result snippets citing the linked sources and are marked [S] (snippet from named source), [A] (anecdotal/vendor claim), or [E] (my estimate). Verify pricing pages before acting.
- Realistic month-6 range for one person at 10-15 hrs/week: $300-2,500/mo for the service candidates; $0-1,000/mo for the product candidates. Nothing here evidences "passive" income at this time budget.

## Candidate 1: QC'd multi-language audio tracks for mid-size YouTube educators
**Offer.** For English-language education/how-to channels (10k-500k subs), deliver reviewed Spanish/Portuguese/Hindi (and other) dubbed audio tracks per video, uploaded via YouTube's multi-language audio feature, with a per-channel terminology glossary and human spot-check of technical terms, names, and jokes. Price per finished minute, monthly retainer per channel.

**Demand signals.**
- YouTube auto-dubbing rolled out to 80M creators in June 2025; "Expressive Speech" added early 2026; 27 languages; averaged 6M+ daily viewers watching at least 10 minutes of auto-dubbed content in Dec 2025 (reported Feb 2026). [S: blog.youtube via metricool/videodubbing/bemultilingual]
- Creators who upload custom multi-language audio tracks see 25%+ of total watch time from non-primary languages. [S: videodubbing.com, 2026, citing YouTube]
- Auto-dubbing is fully automatic inside YouTube; custom tracks are creator-controlled and can carry a human-checked dub. YouTube is piloting lip-sync. [S: storytool.io, sync.so, 2026]

**Why this is not just "YouTube does it for free".** The free auto-dub is the competitor and the proof of demand at once. The sellable delta is: glossary-consistent terminology, brand voice consistency, cloned-voice dubs (with creator consent), and QA on high-value evergreen videos where a bad dub costs retention. If YouTube's Expressive Speech + lip-sync closes that gap, this offer shrinks to QA-only. Treat as a 12-24 month window.

**Automation breakdown (approx. 70% automated).**
- AI end-to-end: transcription, translation, voice-cloned dub generation (ElevenLabs Dubbing Studio; API $0.33/min auto with watermark, $0.50/min without or in Studio [S: elevenlabs.io/pricing/api via fish.audio, happyrobot, geckodub]; Creator plan $22/mo ~40 min automatic dubbing [S]), file rendering, upload via YouTube Studio.
- Human: glossary setup per channel (once), spot-check 5-10% of each dub (names, numbers, jokes), client onboarding and approvals, outreach/sales.
- Tools/cost: ElevenLabs Creator $22/mo or Pro $99/mo; Claude/ChatGPT for glossary + QA summaries $20/mo; optional Descript $24/mo. Total $50-150/mo.

**Economics.** Startup ~$100. Time to first dollar: 2-6 weeks (cold outreach to 50-100 channels, one free pilot video). Month-6 [E]: 2-4 channels x $150-400/mo = $300-1,500/mo. Saturation: medium (many dubbing vendors, but few sell channel-level QC). Policy risk: medium (voice-clone consent; YouTube may make custom tracks unnecessary; disclosure of altered/synthetic audio required).

## Candidate 2: AI UGC ad-variation packs for small Shopify DTC brands
**Offer.** Monthly pack of 10-20 short vertical ad variants (hooks x scripts x AI actors, product-in-frame b-roll) for brands spending $2-20k/mo on Meta/TikTok, delivered as a testing matrix with a one-page hook report. $500-1,500/mo per brand.

**Demand signals.**
- Arcads: Lite $29/mo (no talking actors); Starter $77/mo promo from $110 list, 8,000 credits ~10 one-actor videos; effective cost ~$11/video; 1,000+ AI actors. [S: creatify.ai, eesel.ai, wireflow.ai, layer3labs, 2026]
- Creatify: free 10 credits; Starter $39/mo 100 credits; Pro $99/mo 300 credits; puts real product photo/link into frame. [S: layer3labs, ezugc, hyperfx, 2026]
- At least nine competing AI-UGC tools compared in 2026 roundups (adlibrary, hyperfx) — market is real and crowded on the tool side, which is exactly why brands want someone to run the testing loop.
- Performance (CTR/CPA lift) claims: no verified data found in this session; vendor blogs only [A].

**Automation breakdown (approx. 60%).**
- AI end-to-end: script generation from product page + reviews (Claude/ChatGPT), actor video (Arcads/Creatify), product b-roll (Kling/Veo 3/Runway — pricing not verified this session, "no data found"), captions, resizing (CapCut/Descript), variant naming and export.
- Human: hook strategy and angle selection, brand approval, compliance (health/beauty claims), ad-account read-out of what won, sales.
- Tools/cost: Arcads Starter $77-110 + Creatify $39-99 + Claude $20 + CapCut Pro ~$10 = $150-240/mo (pass through as a client cost).

**Economics.** Startup $150-250. Time to first dollar: 3-8 weeks (needs 1-2 free sample packs as portfolio). Month-6 [E]: 2-3 brands = $1,000-3,000/mo gross, ~$700-2,500 net of tools. Saturation: high on generic "AI UGC agency", medium if you own one product category (e.g., supplements, pet, home). Policy risk: medium — Meta/TikTok require synthetic/AI disclosure on some ad types (rule specifics not verified here), and AI-actor tools tighten likeness terms.

## Candidate 3: Inbound AI missed-call receptionist for plumbing/HVAC (build on Vapi/Retell or white-label)
**Offer.** After-hours and overflow call answering that captures name/address/problem, quotes service windows, books into the shop's calendar or CRM, and texts a summary. Inbound only (no outbound robocalls). $250-500/mo setup-free retainer per shop, plus per-minute pass-through above an allowance.

**Demand signals.**
- Platform base rates: Vapi $0.05/min, Retell $0.07/min (Retell $0.055 voice infra + components); all-in production cost $0.10-0.31/min depending on STT/LLM/TTS stack; Retell ~$0.11-0.15/min with GPT-4o + Deepgram + ElevenLabs. Retell includes 20 concurrent calls, Vapi 10. [S: cloudtalk, cekura, dialora, ainora, retellai blog, Medium/Automation Labs, 2026]
- Agency resale to HVAC/plumbing/roofing: $297-797/mo with 60-80% margins [A: trillet.ai agency blog]; direct-to-business AI receptionists $200-700/mo vs $1,500-3,000/mo human receptionist [A: mostailabs]; white-label VoiceAI Connect $199/mo flat to agencies [S: vendor pricing]; usage-based example $8/hr talk time -> ~$240/mo for 1 hr/day [A].
- Case studies (vendor-reported, unaudited): $17,000 recovered first month; Jobber AI Receptionist booked $4,800 in first month from calls previously going to voicemail. [A]
- Commoditization signal: AI-native receptionists for plumbers start at $29-99/mo entry tiers (as of 2026-07-10) and field-service suites (Jobber) now bundle one. [S: augmentedtrades, vellum]

**Automation breakdown (approx. 55%).**
- AI end-to-end: call handling, transcription, intent capture, calendar/CRM write via API or n8n, SMS summary, daily digest.
- Human: discovery call, script/knowledge base per shop (service area, pricing rules, emergency triage), test calls, telephony setup (Twilio number, forwarding rules), compliance (call-recording consent varies by state; two-party states need disclosure), monthly QA of failed calls, sales.
- Tools/cost: Vapi or Retell pay-as-you-go ($0-50/mo at pilot volume), Twilio number ~$1-2/mo + minutes, n8n cloud $24/mo or self-host, Claude $20. Under $100/mo before clients.

**Economics.** Startup $50-250 (mostly test minutes). Time to first dollar: 4-8 weeks (local outreach, one free 2-week pilot). Month-6 [E]: 3-6 shops x $250-450 = $750-2,700/mo gross; minutes cost $30-120/shop. Saturation: medium (many "AI voice agency" YouTubers push this; local trades are still underserved). Policy risk: medium — inbound only avoids FCC AI-robocall rules; recording-consent and AI-disclosure-on-call laws are state-specific and tightening; suites bundling free receptionists compress price.

## Candidate 4: Personalized photo-based AI children's books on Etsy with print-on-demand
**Offer.** Parent uploads a photo + name + 3 interests; you deliver a 16-24 page illustrated story with the child as hero, digital PDF ($15-25) or printed hardcover via POD ($40-55). Seasonal hooks (birthday, new sibling, first day of school).

**Demand signals.**
- Etsy has dedicated market pages for "personalized ai books", "ai custom book", "ai books", "personalized childrens book" with listings such as ABC books with AI images, superhero books with child photos, "Journey Around the World". [S: etsy.com market pages, observed via search 2026-09]
- Price bands: $2.50 per digital story up to $50+ printed; AI-illustrated printed books $40-55 approaching traditional personalized-book pricing (Wonderbly-class). [S: lullaby.ink 2026 comparison; little-hero.app 2026]
- Listing counts and sales counts: not retrievable (Etsy page fetch blocked) — "no data found".

**Automation breakdown (approx. 65%).**
- AI end-to-end: story text (Claude/ChatGPT with a fixed template), character-consistent illustrations (GPT-image / Midjourney character reference / Flux with LoRA), page layout via Canva template or a small script, PDF export, POD order (Lulu/Printify API).
- Human: face-likeness QC (AI still drifts on children's faces across pages), one proof round with the parent, Etsy listing photos and SEO, customer messaging, refunds.
- Tools/cost: Midjourney $10-30/mo or GPT-image API pay-as-you-go, Claude $20, Canva Pro $15, Etsy listing fees $0.20 + 6.5% transaction + payment fees. $50-80/mo.

**Economics.** Startup $50-150. Time to first dollar: 2-6 weeks (Etsy ranking takes time; first sales often from Facebook groups / Reddit gift threads). Month-6 [E]: 10-40 orders/mo x $20-45 avg = $200-1,500/mo gross, 40-60% margin on printed. Saturation: medium-high (many sellers; differentiate with photo-likeness quality and turnaround). Policy risk: medium — Etsy requires AI-generated content disclosure under its creativity standards (rule not re-verified this session); child-photo handling needs a clear privacy/deletion policy.

## Candidate 5: Vertical n8n workflow templates + paid install for one niche
**Offer.** Pick one vertical the operator can talk to (e.g., podcasters: episode -> transcript -> show notes -> clips -> newsletter -> social; or real-estate agents: lead -> enrichment -> follow-up sequence -> CRM). Sell the template ($49-149) on marketplaces and a $300-800 "installed and tested in your accounts" service on Upwork/own site.

**Demand signals.**
- n8n Markets: 850+ templates listed; Automation Workflows marketplace: seller keeps 85%, no listing fees, Stripe/PayPal/Wise payouts. Template prices $29 simple to $299+ industry-specific. [S: n8nmarkets.com, automationworkflows.io, browseract 2026]
- n8n's official free library has 11,700+ templates — strong price pressure on generic templates. [S: connectsafely.ai 2026]
- Upwork: n8n specialists bill $40-100/hr in 2026; entry $25-40/hr; Upwork "n8n experts" and "n8n freelance jobs" pages active (Sep 2026). Freelancers using AI earn 34% more per hour (Upwork stat via remoteaitools). [S]
- Passive $3,200/mo from 5 templates — single Medium post, unverified. [A]

**Automation breakdown (approx. 50%).**
- AI end-to-end: workflow drafting (Claude Code / n8n AI builder), documentation, README and demo video script, listing copy.
- Human: choosing the vertical, talking to 5-10 practitioners, debugging client credentials/APIs, support, Upwork proposals.
- Tools/cost: n8n cloud Starter ~$24/mo (or self-host $5-10/mo VPS), Claude $20, Loom free. Under $50/mo.

**Economics.** Startup $0-50. Time to first dollar: 2-6 weeks via Upwork installs; template marketplace sales are slow and lumpy. Month-6 [E]: 2-4 installs/mo x $300-600 + $100-300 templates = $700-2,500/mo. Saturation: high for generic "automation expert"; medium for a named vertical with a demo. Policy risk: low.

## Candidate 6: Vibe-coded niche micro-tool sold on the web (Stripe), not the App Store
**Offer.** One narrow paid utility for a community the operator belongs to (calculators, generators, checkers, planners), built with Lovable/Bolt/Claude Code, $5-15/mo or $19-49 one-time, distributed via Reddit/Discord/SEO and TikTok demos.

**Demand signals.**
- Named evidence: Forbes (2026-03-23) profiles solo founders vibe-coding digital products with revenue; Lovable reported ~$200M ARR in 12 months then ~$400M within another four months (secondary sources). [S: forbes.com, sorceress.games]
- "Vibe coding market $4.7B, 92% of US developers use AI coding tools daily" [S: taskade, findskill — market-report style numbers, provenance not verified].
- Individual stories ("$6,200 MRR by month four", "$800K ARR in nine months", Pieter Levels' game at $138k/mo Nov 2025): the Levels figure is self-reported by a known founder; the others are unnamed marketing anecdotes [A].
- Exit price bands for vibe-coded apps exist (exitbid.io) — a secondary market is forming; numbers not retrieved.
- App Store: reports that Apple has moved to reject/ban low-effort AI-built apps under spam rules [S: findskill.ai; specifics "no data found"]. This is why the recommendation is web distribution.

**Automation breakdown (approx. 60%).**
- AI end-to-end: build, landing page, Stripe integration scaffolding, docs, SEO copy.
- Human: picking a problem with real pull, shipping fixes, support, distribution posts, pricing tests.
- Tools/cost: Lovable $25/mo or Claude Pro/Code $20-100/mo, Supabase/Vercel free tiers, domain $12/yr, Stripe 2.9%+30c. $30-120/mo.

**Economics.** Startup $50-150. Time to first dollar: 4-12 weeks. Month-6 [E]: $0-1,000/mo, very high variance; median outcome is near zero without distribution effort. Saturation: high for generic tools; low-medium within a specific hobby/professional community. Policy risk: low on web; medium-high on App Store.

## Candidate 7 (bridge, not a hustle): Domain AI-evaluation gigs on Mercor / Outlier / DataAnnotation
**Offer.** Sell hours of human judgment: RLHF preference rating, coding or writing evaluation, red-teaming.

**Demand signals.**
- Rates: national average AI-trainer freelance pay $31/hr (early 2026); entry $12-25/hr; DataAnnotation $15-45/hr; Outlier $15-80/hr (experts to $200+); Mercor $25-250+/hr, RLHF annotator $40-55/hr, senior coding evaluator $85-110/hr, legal/medical $90-160/hr. [S: mercor.com, aigigjobs, hirefeed, remotestack, careerseeker 2026]
- Key caveat from the same sources: work availability is inconsistent ("a $90/hr platform that gives you 6 hours a week pays less than $55/hr with 22"). Without a credential the operator lands in the $15-30/hr generalist tier.

**Automation breakdown (approx. 5%).** Using AI to complete tasks violates platform terms and gets accounts banned; only admin (tracking, invoicing) is automatable. Included because it is real, current, and fundable within weeks — not because it fits the "lean on AI" brief.

**Economics.** Startup $0. Time to first dollar: 1-4 weeks (assessments, onboarding queues). Month-6 [E]: $500-2,000/mo at 10-15 hrs/week if work is available; can drop to $0 during droughts. Saturation: high (queues, project pauses). Policy risk: low, but zero AI-assist tolerance.

## Saturated or restricted in this lens
- **AI music to streaming (Suno/Udio -> Spotify/Deezer).** Deezer: 75,000 fully-AI tracks/day (44% of uploads) in Apr 2026, 90,000/day in Jun 2026, >50% of daily uploads by Jul 2026 (TechCrunch 2026-07-21); AI tracks are labeled and excluded from editorial and algorithmic recommendations; 85% of AI-music streams flagged fraudulent and demonetized [S: TechCrunch, imusician, labelgrid, dynamoi]. Spotify (policy 2025-09-25, unchanged as of Mar 2026): AI allowed with DDEX disclosure, aggressive spam filter, unauthorized voice clones removed [S: TechCrunch, dynamoi]. Verdict: royalties from volume uploads are effectively gone; only sync/direct-license sales remain (not researched — "no data found").
- **AI music / beat-compilation YouTube channels.** YouTube renamed "repetitious" to "inauthentic content" in July 2025 targeting mass-produced templated content; by Jan 2026 it had terminated 11 channels and wiped 6 others (35M combined subs, 4.7B views) [S: air.io timeline, moshion, lenspov]. "Generic beat compilations and raw Suno/Udio dumps are dead" for monetization; AI music channels can still be monetized only with disclosure, original commentary/editing, and passing reused-content + Content ID checks [S: outlierkit 2026]. Verdict: restricted; a human-hosted, story-driven music channel is a different (slow) business.
- **Fully AI-generated podcasts.** ~35.4% of new Spotify podcast feeds were AI-generated as of early May 2026; Spotify extended verified badges to podcasts and bans impersonation; no comprehensive policy on fully-AI shows yet [S: Variety 2026, MBW]. Spotify says sponsorships are its fastest-growing podcast monetization (+100% YoY) — but that accrues to shows with audiences. NotebookLM Audio Overviews are free and Google states no plans to monetize them [S]. Verdict: saturated on the open feed; a private/branded B2B podcast service is untested ("no data found").
- **GPT Store builder payouts.** Revenue share never broadly launched; as of early 2026 it remains an invite-only pilot for a small group of US builders; anecdotal ceiling $100-500/mo for most; realistic money is in building internal GPTs for companies ($5-20k setup claims are vendor/consultant anecdotes) [S/A: wildnetedge, digitalapplied, OpenAI community thread].
- **Claude skills / Anthropic marketplace.** Anthropic Marketplace is for enterprise customers with committed annual spend buying from partners like Snowflake, Harvey, Replit (no commission) — not open to solo builders [S: thenextweb, claude.com]. Third-party "Claude skills" storefronts (Agensi 30% fee, Claude Protocol from $3.99, KissMySkills $14.99) exist but no sales-volume data found; treat as speculative [S].
- **Generic "AI automation agency" / "AI voice agency"** as pitched by YouTube gurus: real underlying demand (see Candidates 3 and 5) but the generic positioning is saturated on Upwork and in local outreach; only vertical + integration depth is differentiated.
- **Free-feature erosion to watch:** YouTube auto-dubbing (Expressive Speech, lip-sync pilot), Jobber/field-service suites bundling AI receptionists ($29-99 tiers), n8n's 11,700+ free templates. Each compresses the low end of Candidates 1, 3, and 5.

## TL;DR

- Best risk-adjusted fit for a $500 / 10-15 h/wk solo operator: **marketplace flipping with an AI listing pipeline** (eBay hard goods, Depop clothing at 0% commission) - first dollar in 1-2 weeks, ~40% automatable, no platform AI-content risk. See [[research/candidates/ebay-hard-goods-flipping-ai-listing]] and [[research/candidates/depop-thrift-flipping-ai-listing]].
- **TikTok Shop affiliate** is the biggest evidenced demand pool (US GMV $15.1B in 2025, +68%; avg affiliate commission ~13%) but the money-making part (a real human on camera) is the part AI cannot do: AI visuals must be labeled, AI voices/avatars are banned from LIVE as of 2026-07-31, and avatars may not claim first-hand use. Use AI for scripting/selection/editing only (the "70/30" pattern).
- **Amazon Merch on Demand changed the game on 2026-06-01**: organic-only sellers were cut to $2.44 per $19.99 tee (Creator tier); 15%+ external traffic doubles it (Plus, 2x) and 35%+ pays 2.16x (Premium). Merch is now only worth doing as a social/Pinterest traffic play, and approval reportedly runs 30-40%.
- **Etsy now requires AI disclosure** (Jan 2026: "Designed by" attribution + disclosure line) and since 2025-06-10 requires original design; Q1 2026 enforcement hit undisclosed AI POD and digital-download sellers hardest. Fee stack ~9.75% + $0.45 before the 12-15% Offsite Ads fee. Disclosed, niche POD (tabletop: tarot/puzzles/cards) still viable.
- **China-sourced dropshipping is effectively dead for micro-sellers**: de minimis ended for China 2025-05, for all countries 2025-08-29 (extended Feb 2026). TikTok also demands a $500 minimum campaign / $50 per day ad group; AI-UGC ads convert 8-22% worse on $100+ products. Only US-supplier or POD-fulfilled variants survive, and they eat the whole $500.
- **Rank-and-rent** still works for map-pack/phone-call niches (rents $500-3,000/mo; HVAC CPL ~$105, plumbing $55-183) but AI Overviews now cover ~60% of informational queries, time-to-rent is 4-9 months, and Google Business Profile rules on lead-gen listings are the policy landmine.
- Platform fee reality 2026: eBay ~13.25-13.6% + $0.30-0.40; Poshmark 20% ($2.95 under $15); FB Marketplace 10% shipped / 0% local; Depop 0% + 3.3% + $0.45; Etsy 6.5% + 3% + $0.25 + $0.20; TikTok Shop 8% (up from 6% on 2026-08-04, 3% for new sellers' first 30 days); Amazon FBA 25-35% all-in plus a 3.5% fuel surcharge from 2026-04-17.
- Caveat: every figure here comes from 2026 secondary sources surfaced by search; the egress proxy blocked all page fetches, so platform pages (fee schedules, policy text) were NOT read directly. Verify before acting. Whatnot, Amazon Influencer Program and Etsy digital-only printables: no data found (search budget exhausted).

## Method and evidence quality

16 web searches on 2026-09-10; every WebFetch (including ebay.com, help.etsy.com, seller-us.tiktok.com, merch.amazon.com, printful.com, printify.com, shopify.com) was blocked by the org egress policy, and the session's WebSearch budget ended before follow-ups on Whatnot, Amazon Influencer, Etsy printables, Reddit income reports and POD tool pricing pages. Evidence tiers used below:

- **Verified-ish (V)**: fee schedules or policy details repeated consistently across 3+ independent 2026 sources that cite the platform.
- **Reported (R)**: a single 2026 industry blog or a blog citing a research house (e.g., Momentum Works via Axis Intelligence).
- **Anecdotal (A)**: creator-earnings tiers, guru claims, "what works" blog posts.
- Tool prices marked "approx." are general knowledge, not verified this session.

## TikTok Shop affiliate: AI-scripted, phone-filmed 30-second "problem/solution" clips for $15-40 beauty and home products from the free-sample program

**Offer/format.** Join TikTok Shop Affiliate, request free samples for products meeting a filter (commission >= 20%, 4.5+ stars, visible sales velocity), film 2-3 short talking-head or hands-only demo clips per day, post with product link. AI handles selection, script, hook variants, captions, and posting cadence; a human is on camera because of policy (below).

**Demand signals.**
- US TikTok Shop GMV $15.1B in 2025, up 68% from $9B in 2024 (R, Axis Intelligence / AutoFaceless citing Momentum Works, 2026).
- Average US affiliate commission 13.02%; open-plan rates 5% (electronics) to 30% (beauty/wellness); targeted rates 15-50% (R, Hamster Garage and ShortFormNation, 2026).
- Closed-loop conversion averages 4.7% vs 2-4% for classic ecommerce; affiliate-link engagement 5.2% (R, Hamster Garage, 2026).
- ~11.3M creators generated TikTok Shop affiliate sales globally in 2026, ~945K in the US (R, Affiverse Media, 2026) - that is also the competition count.
- Earnings by tier: nano (1-10K followers) $200-1,500/mo; micro $500-5,000; top 1% average $600K annual GMV, top 5% $80K GMV (A, Branvas / InfluencerFee, 2026). No distribution data on the median affiliate; assume most earn near zero.

**Platform/AI rules (V across Affiverse, Dropship-Spy, Moras, ShortFormNation, Bunny Honey Club, 2026).**
- AI as a production tool (scripts, captions, shot lists) is exempt from labeling. AI-generated visuals or audio (avatars, synthetic voice, generated product demos) must be labeled and are subject to reach suppression.
- AI avatars may not fabricate endorsement, first-hand use, expertise or identity ("I use this every day" is not allowed from an avatar).
- LIVE: AI-generated voices, pre-recorded audio, and static images over 50% of the screen banned effective 2026-07-31.
- Virtual-influencer accounts are barred from Creator Rewards (affiliate commissions are seller-paid so still possible, but reach-suppressed).
- Commission clawbacks on refunds; payout timing after return window (R, Dashboardly, 2026).

**Automation breakdown (~55%).**
- AI end-to-end: product scoring (n8n/Make + Claude reading the affiliate marketplace export, ~$0-24/mo), script + 5 hook variants (Claude/ChatGPT $20/mo), auto-captions and cuts (CapCut, free-$10/mo approx.), post scheduling, comment-reply drafts, weekly performance sheet.
- Human: applying for samples, unboxing/filming (5-8 h/wk), reading comments for claims compliance, choosing what to keep filming.
- Not allowed to automate: the on-camera person, LIVEs.

**Economics.** Startup ~$80-120 (phone tripod/light, one AI subscription). Fees: none to the affiliate. First dollar: 2-6 weeks (follower threshold to join reportedly ~1K; verify). Month-6 realistic: $0-500/mo for most; $200-1,500 per the anecdotal nano band. Saturation: high (945K US affiliates). Policy risk: medium (labeling, claims, shifting rules).

## TikTok Shop first-party seller: one US-sourced consumable, affiliates as the only ad channel

**Offer/format.** Open a TikTok Shop, list a single low-return-risk consumable (e.g., a US-made kitchen or pet item bought wholesale), set a 20-25% open-collab commission so affiliates do the content, run zero paid ads. AI handles listing copy, images, affiliate outreach messages, sample-request triage, and customer-service replies.

**Demand signals.**
- Same $15.1B US GMV (R). Momentum Works counted 803,500 US TikTok Shops in 2025; more than half recorded no sales at all; just over 2,000 cleared $1M GMV (R, Axis Intelligence, 2026). That is the honest base rate.
- Referral fee rose from 6% to 8% on 2026-08-04 for most non-food categories; selected beauty and electronics sub-categories 9.5-11%; new sellers who make a first sale within 60 days get 3% for 30 days (V, Delzonic / WinningHunter / Podbase, 2026).
- Sellers set affiliate commission; open-collab baseline 10-15% (R, ShortFormNation, 2026).

**Automation breakdown (~50%).** AI: listing, images (Ideogram/Midjourney ~$8-20/mo approx.), affiliate DM sequences (n8n + Claude), CS macros, inventory reorder alerts. Human: supplier sourcing and QC, packing/shipping every order (or paying a 3PL), sample approvals, refund disputes. TikTok's fulfillment SLA penalties make this a daily-attention business, not a set-and-forget one.

**Economics.** Startup ~$450-500 (inventory $300, samples to affiliates $60-100, mailers). Take rate: 8% referral + 15-25% affiliate + shipping, so a $25 item nets roughly $10-13 before COGS. First dollar: 3-8 weeks. Month-6 realistic: -$200 to +$1,000 (bimodal; most shops make nothing). Saturation: high. Policy risk: medium (fee changes, product-listing compliance, shipping SLAs).

## Amazon Merch on Demand as an external-traffic play: micro-niche typography tees pushed from Pinterest and TikTok to hit the Plus tier

**Offer/format.** Design hobby/occupation-humor typography shirts (AI-drafted concepts, human-arranged in Kittl/Canva), upload to Merch, and drive 15%+ of sales from your own Pinterest pins and 15-second TikTok clips so royalties double.

**Demand signals and the June 2026 shock.**
- On 2026-06-01 Amazon replaced flat royalties with three tiers: Creator (default) pays $2.44 on a $19.99 tee; Plus pays 2x when 15%+ of trailing-two-month sales come from external traffic; Premium pays 2.16x at 35%+; groups recalculated monthly (V, Merch Titans / MyDesigns / AMZ Prep, 2026). Organic-only sellers saw roughly a 50% royalty cut.
- Application approval reportedly 30-40% in 2026, down after AI-spam waves (A, StoresAutomation, 2026). Tiered design-slot system still applies (slot counts not verified this session).
- AI designs: not banned, but must clear IP/content policy and need non-trivial human contribution (composition, text, color, arrangement) (R, Merch Titans, MerchBanao, 2026).

**Automation breakdown (~70%).** AI: niche/keyword ideation, slogan generation, image generation, mockups, pin descriptions, TikTok scripts, upload metadata, a Pinterest scheduler. Human: final design curation and IP screening (trademark check on every phrase), the Merch application, periodic tier review. External-traffic content can be mostly AI-generated (Pinterest has no equivalent labeling burden as of the sources found; verify).

**Economics.** Startup $0-40/mo (Midjourney ~$10, Kittl/Canva ~$15 approx.). First dollar: 1-3 months (approval wait + first sales). Month-6 realistic: $0-150/mo; a Plus-tier seller with 50 live designs selling 40 units/mo would gross ~$195. Saturation: high. Policy risk: medium-high (approval, IP takedowns, tier rules can change again).

## FB Marketplace and thrift to eBay: hard-goods flipping with eBay Magical Listing plus Flyp cross-listing

**Offer/format.** Source under-priced hard goods locally (game consoles, KitchenAid mixers, vintage audio, tools, small appliances) via Facebook Marketplace saved searches and thrift runs; photograph on a plain backdrop; let eBay's Magical Listing draft title/specifics/description from photos; cross-list to FB shipped and Mercari via Flyp.

**Demand signals.**
- eBay final value fee ~13.25-13.6% up to $7,500 plus $0.30 (orders <= $10) / $0.40 per order (V, Webgility / Frooition / Plott, 2026).
- eBay Magical Listing is free to all sellers (V, EcomAI Daily / FlowLister, 2026).
- Flyp: 100 days free then $9/mo unlimited (6 platforms); List Perfectly $29-249/mo with AI gated at $99+; Vendoo scales to $149.99/mo plus $11.99 add-on bundle (V, Vendoo blog / Voolist / FlipSail / Underpriced, 2026).
- FB Marketplace: 0% for local sales, 10% ($0.80 min) for shipped checkout (V, ListingGenie / SellerFeeCalc, 2026).
- Solo-reseller income reports: no data found this session (Reddit blocked). Treat all profit estimates as operator math, not evidence.

**Automation breakdown (~40%).**
- AI: sourcing alerts (n8n scraping saved searches or IFTTT-style alerts; Claude ranks by comp price), comps lookup, listing copy and item specifics (Magical Listing), background cleanup, cross-listing and auto-delist (Flyp $9), offer-response rules, buyer-message drafts, bookkeeping sheet.
- Human: driving to pick up, testing/cleaning, photographing, packing and shipping, returns. This is the ceiling on automation; it is also why it is not saturated by bots.

**Economics.** Startup $250-350 (inventory float $200-300, mailers/tape, Flyp free at first). Margins: buy at 25-35% of eBay sold comps; after 14% fees and shipping, target 40-50% net on sale price. First dollar: 1-2 weeks. Month-6 realistic: $300-1,000/mo profit at 10-15 h/wk (estimate; unverified). Saturation: medium. Policy risk: low (no AI-content rules; watch category gates and INAD returns).

## Thrift to Depop: Y2K and vintage clothing flipping with AI listing and 0% commission

**Offer/format.** Source branded/vintage clothing at thrift outlets and bins, list on Depop first (0% commission), cross-list to Poshmark and eBay with Flyp, use AI for measurements-to-copy, styling captions and photo cleanup.

**Demand signals.**
- Depop charges eligible US sellers $0 commission plus 3.3% + $0.45 processing; Poshmark takes 20% ($2.95 flat under $15); on a $50 item with $5 shipping Poshmark costs $10.00, Mercari $5.50, Depop ~$2.27 (V, SellerFeeCalc / Voolist / EcomCalcTools, 2026).
- Poshmark can still win when its buyer base pays a higher price (R, SellerFeeCalc, 2026), hence cross-listing rather than choosing.
- Secondhand-market size (ThredUp 2026 report): no data found (search budget).

**Automation breakdown (~40%).** AI: brand/era identification from photos (Claude vision), pricing comps, listing text with size/measurement templates, background removal (Vendoo/Canva), cross-list + auto-delist, relist cadence, offer auto-accept rules. Human: sourcing (the skill that decides profit), measuring, photographing, shipping.

**Economics.** Startup $150-250 (inventory $120-200, mailers, Flyp free trial). First dollar: 1-2 weeks. Month-6 realistic: $200-800/mo profit at 10-15 h/wk (estimate; no verified income data). Saturation: medium (crowded, but fee arbitrage is real in 2026). Policy risk: low.

## Etsy tabletop print-on-demand hybrid: original tarot/oracle decks, jigsaw puzzles and collectible cards with a digital companion download, AI-assisted and disclosed

**Offer/format.** Build a small original deck or puzzle art series (your own prompts, human-curated and edited, disclosed as "Designed by"), fulfil via Printify/Printful, and bundle a digital companion (guidebook PDF, printable spread sheets) so each order includes an instant-download upsell.

**Demand signals.**
- Marmalead (2026) names custom tarot cards, personalized collectible cards and POD jigsaw puzzles as the most profitable, fastest-growing "high-passion tabletop" POD products; saturated categories run 15-20% margins (R).
- Etsy fee stack: $0.20 listing, 6.5% transaction on price + shipping, 3% + $0.25 processing; Offsite Ads 15% (12% once $10K trailing revenue, at which point enrollment is mandatory) (V, Craftybase / Marmalead / Gelato, 2026).
- Sellers who set their own price (Printify/Etsy) commonly net 30-60% vs 10-20% Redbubble royalties; a $25 tee nets $10-15 on Printify vs $3-5 on Redbubble (R, EcommerceCEO / Merch Titans, 2026).
- Listing counts for tarot/puzzle POD on Etsy: no data found (site blocked).

**Policy (V across BulkMockup, ShieldMyShop, Ngini, IsCompliant, ListyBox, Artomate, 2026).**
- 2025-06-10: Creativity Standards edited to remove "templated designs" allowance; items must be based on the seller's original design.
- Jan 2026: listings using generative AI must be marked as AI-assisted and attributed "Designed by [seller]" instead of "Made by", with a disclosure line in the description; undisclosed use can be removed without warning.
- Allowed only when the seller wrote the prompts and directed the output; bought prompt packs are not.
- Q1 2026 enforcement concentrated on undisclosed AI POD artwork, AI-visual digital downloads, and mass ChatGPT descriptions (R).

**Automation breakdown (~65%).** AI: concept and series ideation, art generation (Midjourney/Ideogram ~$10-20/mo), upscaling, mockups, listing copy with disclosure boilerplate, guidebook text, SEO tags, Pinterest pins, order routing (Printify auto-fulfil). Human: curation and editing of each card/image (required for policy), sample-order QC, customer questions, occasional re-prints.

**Economics.** Startup $100-160 (30 listings $6, one sample deck/puzzle $40-80, AI subs). Margin: a $45 puzzle with ~$22 base cost nets ~$16 after Etsy fees, ~$9 if an Offsite Ad triggered. First dollar: 2-8 weeks. Month-6 realistic: $0-600/mo. Saturation: medium. Policy risk: medium (disclosure enforcement, IP on any recognisable style).

## Rank-and-rent emergency-service microsites in secondary metros (24-hour plumber, water-damage restoration, tree removal), AI-built and rented to one contractor

**Offer/format.** Build a 15-25 page city-plus-service site with Claude-written content, real local detail, call tracking and a lead form; rank it for map-pack-adjacent "emergency X near me" queries in a 100-300K population metro; rent it to a licensed contractor for a flat monthly fee tied to tracked calls.

**Demand signals.**
- Rents commonly $500-3,000/mo per ranked site (R, Ippei / LocusPilot, 2026); anchored to documented lead volume x conservative per-lead value, priced just under the renter's blended CPA (R, RankLocal, 2026).
- Google Ads CPL benchmarks: HVAC ~$105; plumbing $55-183 depending on term; CA/NY/FL/TX run 20-50% above national (R, LocusPilot citing SearchLight Digital and AgedLeadStore, 2025-2026).
- AI Overviews default on ~60% of US informational queries, compressing informational CTR, while local commercial "near me" and map-pack clicks still convert (R, RankLocal / TheLeadGuy, 2026). Consensus of the 2026 guides: the shortcut version is declining; quality sites still rent.
- Guru claim of $100K/mo (Ryan Doser) is anecdotal marketing; ignore for planning.

**Automation breakdown (~70%).** AI: keyword and city selection, site generation (Astro/WordPress + Claude), service-page copy, schema, image generation, citation submissions via n8n, monthly rank/call reports, outreach email drafts. Human: choosing a defensible niche/city, getting a phone number and call tracking, any Google Business Profile decision (see risk), and the sales conversation with the contractor, plus a real contract.

**Economics.** Startup $150-300 (domain ~$12, hosting $5-10/mo, call tracking ~$20-45/mo approx., citations $0-100). First dollar: 4-9 months (ranking lag, then a renter). Month-6 realistic: $0-500/mo (one early renter at best). Saturation: low-medium in secondary metros. Policy risk: high - Google Business Profile guidelines restrict listings for businesses without a real, staffed location and for lead-gen fronts (not fetched this session; verify current text before creating any GBP), plus state licensing-advertising rules for trades and FTC lead-gen disclosure expectations.

## US-supplier dropshipping with AI-generated UGC ads (flagged: capital-constrained, near-restricted)

**Offer/format.** Shopify store, single product from a US-warehoused supplier (Zendrop/Spocket-type), ads made with AI UGC tools, tested on TikTok/Meta.

**Demand signals and constraints.**
- De minimis ended for China/HK shipments in May 2025 and for all countries on 2025-08-29, continued by executive order in Feb 2026; every low-value parcel now needs duties and entry paperwork, hitting AliExpress/DSers-style dropshippers hardest (V, Syncee / Practical Ecommerce / TariffDesk / Stickiply, 2025-2026).
- TikTok Ads: $500 minimum campaign budget and $50 minimum daily ad-group budget; ~25 conversion events per ad group per week to exit learning, which at a $25 CPA implies ~$90/day (R, Stackmatix / TikAdTools, 2026).
- AI UGC tools cost $29-110/mo; human UGC $150-500 per video. AI UGC can hit ~3.8x ROAS in the best cases but converts 8-14% worse than human UGC above $100 price points, widening to 18-22% above $200; generic AliExpress clips cost $20+ CPM vs ~$8 for native UGC (R, ProductLair / Lightfall / MyUGC, 2026). Realistic target 2-3x ROAS; product viability visible in 3-5 days and $100-200 spend (R).
- Solo-seller P&Ls: no data found beyond vendor blogs.

**Automation breakdown (~60%).** AI: product research scraping, store build (Shopify AI + Claude), product copy, AI UGC variants, ad copy, CS macros, order routing via supplier app. Human: supplier vetting, ad-account setup and daily kill/scale decisions, chargebacks, returns policy.

**Economics.** Startup exceeds the budget: Shopify ~$39/mo (approx.), supplier app $30-50/mo, AI UGC $29-110/mo, plus TikTok's $500 campaign minimum. First dollar: 1-4 weeks if a product hits. Month-6 realistic: -$500 to +$500. Saturation: high. Policy risk: medium-high (tariffs, ad-account bans, TikTok AI-label rules on ads that look like creator UGC).

## Saturated or restricted in this lens

- **AliExpress/China dropshipping to US consumers**: de minimis gone (China 2025-05; all countries 2025-08-29; extended Feb 2026); per-parcel duties and entry paperwork make sub-$30 items uneconomic (V). Restricted in practice.
- **AI-avatar and AI-voice TikTok Shop affiliate content**: mandatory labeling with reach suppression; avatars cannot claim first-hand use; AI voices/pre-recorded audio banned from LIVE since 2026-07-31; virtual influencers excluded from Creator Rewards (V). The "faceless AI affiliate" staple is policy-restricted.
- **Undisclosed AI art on Etsy (POD or digital downloads)**: Jan 2026 disclosure rule plus 2025-06-10 originality rule; Q1 2026 removals concentrated here (V/R). Prompt-pack resale is explicitly disallowed.
- **Amazon Merch relying on Amazon organic traffic**: royalty cut ~50% to $2.44 per $19.99 tee from 2026-06-01; approval ~30-40% (V/A). Saturated and de-monetized unless you bring traffic.
- **Redbubble/TeePublic passive POD**: 10-20% royalties, $3-5 per $25 tee vs $10-15 self-priced (R). Saturated, low margin; use only as a free extra channel.
- **Amazon online arbitrage under $500**: fees 25-35% of price plus a 3.5% fuel/logistics surcharge from 2026-04-17 and Jan-15-2026 fee increases (V, AMZ Prep / Amazon Selling Partners / Feedvisor); inventory float and brand gating make it capital-constrained for this operator.
- **Paid-ad dropshipping generally**: TikTok $500 minimum campaign; AI-UGC conversion penalty on higher-ticket items (R). Saturated.
- **Not researched (no data found)**: Whatnot/eBay Live live-selling fees and seller stats; Amazon Influencer Program onsite commissions; Etsy digital-only printables listing counts; Reddit income reports for part-time resellers; POD tool pricing pages (Printify/Printful/Shopify blocked).

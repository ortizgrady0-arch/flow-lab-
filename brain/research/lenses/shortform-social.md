## TL;DR

- Best risk-adjusted bets in this lens are B2B services where AI does 60-75% of the work and a human supplies the "authenticity" every platform now rewards: LinkedIn founder ghostwriting (entry retainers $1,200-$2,000/mo for 4-6 posts), a vertical-niche local-business short-form package ($500-$1,000/mo), and productized AI-UGC ad-variant batches for DTC brands (Fiverr market observed at $5-$180/gig; Creatify $39-$99/mo). See [[research/candidates/linkedin-founder-ghostwriting]], [[research/candidates/local-vertical-shortform-package]], [[research/candidates/ai-ugc-ad-variant-batches]].
- TikTok Shop affiliate is the largest pure-platform opportunity (US GMV $15.1B in 2025, affiliate content ~42% of US GMV, average US commission ~13%) but earnings are extremely skewed (top 0.5% of creators drive 38% of affiliate GMV) and AI use is constrained: AIGC label mandatory, AI voices banned in Shop LIVEs since 2026-05-23, virtual influencers cannot make experience claims, and some brands (SharkNinja) ban AI affiliate content and claw back commissions.
- Whop clipping is real money but thin: $0.20-$6 per 1,000 views (average near $1), ~98,000 registered clippers competing for >$1M/month of brand spend; independently tracked payouts of $2.58M across 8,466 earners imply roughly $300 lifetime per earner. Treat it as a cash-flow warm-up, not a business.
- Every platform moved against low-effort AI in 2025-26: YouTube "inauthentic content" (renamed July 2025; January 2026 wave terminated channels with 35M combined subscribers; 3-strike system), Meta unoriginal-content crackdown (2025-07-15; ~500k accounts actioned, ~10M impersonation profiles removed), LinkedIn Authenticity Update (March 2026; generic AI posts reportedly lose up to 47% reach). Pure-AI faceless Shorts farms and repost meme pages are now the worst bets here.
- Shorts AdSense is not a business: Shorts RPM is roughly $0.01-$0.20 per 1,000 views (finance/tech about 10x lifestyle); 1M Shorts views/month is ~$50-$150. Faceless Shorts only make sense as a funnel for affiliate or lead-gen with human-written scripts.
- Brand budgets are rising into 2026: influencer marketing ~$32.55B global, 87.49% of surveyed brands expect budget increases, nano/micro and UGC creators are the fastest-growing tiers, TikTok is the top platform for increased spend (Influencer Marketing Hub 2026 benchmark, via secondary reporting).
- Human UGC average price fell to ~$198 per deliverable (Billo data, 2025-26) as AI-UGC tools entered; the opening for a solo operator is ad-creative testing volume (10-20 AI hook variations per batch), not replacing hero UGC.
- Evidence caveat: this session's WebSearch budget hit its cap after 18 queries and nearly every primary domain (TikTok, YouTube, Whop, Meta, Fiverr, IMH, tool pricing pages) was egress-blocked for fetch, so figures below are from 2025-26 search-result summaries of third-party articles plus a few press reports. Re-verify official pages before committing money.

## How to read the evidence grades

- **Verified**: platform/official statement or reputable press (TechCrunch, Forbes, Tubefilter) or a price observed directly in a marketplace listing title.
- **Survey/analytics**: third-party analytics or survey (Momentum Works, Tabcut, Clutch, Metricool, Influencer Marketing Hub) relayed by a secondary article.
- **Anecdotal**: blog/guru/forum/SEO-article estimates. Useful for ranges, not for planning.

Operator assumptions for all estimates: one US person, 10-15 hrs/week, under $500 capital, no audience, comfortable with Claude/ChatGPT, image/video/voice generators, n8n/Make/Zapier. See [[research/lenses/index]] for the other lenses.

## TikTok Shop affiliate: hands-on demo clips in one sub-$40 product category

**Offer.** Post 1-2 short (15-45s) hands-on demo/review clips per day of inexpensive products in one category (kitchen gadgets, cleaning, phone accessories, pet supplies) with TikTok Shop affiliate links; request free samples through open collaborations; later negotiate targeted-collaboration rates and Spark Ads usage fees. Human hands/face on camera; AI does everything around the shoot.

**Demand evidence.**
- US TikTok Shop GMV $15.1B in 2025, up 68% from ~$9B in 2024; 2026 projection $23.4B (survey/analytics via Axis Intelligence, resourcera, hamstergarage, 2026).
- Affiliate creator content drives ~42% of US GMV, the single largest channel; 800,000+ US creators actively monetizing through the affiliate program (survey/analytics via hamstergarage/syncly, 2026).
- Concentration: top 0.5% of creators (~4,000 accounts) drive 38% of affiliate GMV; top 1% of affiliates average ~$600k annual GMV (survey/analytics, 2026).
- Commission: average US rate 13.02% and rising; beauty 15-30%, fashion 10-15%; open collaboration defaults 10-15%, targeted deals 25-50% (survey/analytics via influencerfee, hamstergarage, 2026). Brands net ~67.3% of GMV after 6% platform fee, samples, returns; affiliates face clawbacks on returns (dashboardly, hamstergarage, 2026).
- Earnings by tier (anecdotal, branvas 2026): nano 1k-10k followers $200-$1,500/mo; micro 10k-100k $500-$5,000/mo.
- Brand side: TikTok is the highest-incidence platform among brands increasing influencer spend (32%) (IMH 2026 via archive.com).
- Entry requirement widely reported as 5,000 followers and 18+ for the US affiliate program; not verified this session.

**Automation breakdown (est. 50%).**
- AI end-to-end: product/trend research (Kalodata or EchoTik-type analytics, ~$30-$100/mo, price not verified), hook and script generation (Claude/ChatGPT $20/mo), on-screen text and captions, auto-editing templates (CapCut Pro ~$10-$20/mo, not verified), posting schedule, comment-reply drafts, weekly performance summaries via n8n.
- Human required: filming with real hands/face (experience claims such as "I use this daily" are only allowed from a real person), sample requests and brand messaging, replying to buyer questions, deciding what to promote, LIVE selling (must be real-time human voice since 2026-05-23).
- Not allowed or risky: fully synthetic presenter testimonials; unlabeled AI voice; AI content for brands that ban it (SharkNinja publicly did, with commission removal).

**Economics.** Startup ~$100-$250 (tripod/light, CapCut, Claude; samples are free). Time to first dollar 6-12 weeks (follower threshold plus first conversions). Realistic month-6: $0-$800/mo, heavily skewed; many accounts earn near zero. Saturation: high (800k+ US affiliates). Policy risk: medium (AIGC label, LIVE AI-voice ban, Creator Health Rating penalties, brand-specific AI bans, clawbacks).

**Verdict.** Worth running as a content-flywheel alongside a service business, not as the primary income plan. Cross-ref [[research/candidates/tiktok-shop-affiliate-demo-clips]].

## AI-UGC ad-variant batches for DTC brands (productized creative testing)

**Offer.** "10 AI-avatar hook variations of your winning ad in 48 hours" at $150-$300 per batch, sold to small Shopify/DTC brands and media buyers running TikTok and Meta paid ads; upsell a $500-$1,000/mo creative-testing retainer. Output is labeled synthetic ads for paid placement in the brand's ad account (not organic affiliate posts).

**Demand evidence.**
- Fiverr "AI UGC" gigs observed in listing titles (verified prices, 2026): $5, $10, $10, $25, $30, $35, $180 ("realistic AI UGC video ads for ecom brands"). Fiverr reportedly launched a dedicated AI Video Hub on 2026-03-23 (playcut, not verified).
- Tool pricing: Creatify free tier, $39/mo Starter, $99/mo Pro (Creatify blog, 2026). Arcads has no public pricing (pricing page returned 404 in July 2026); third parties quote ~$110-$220/mo, top tier ~$385-$550/mo (eesel, superscale, 2026). Entry pricing comparison: Creatify $19, Zeely $25, Arcads $110, MagicUGC $149 (cometly/argil, 2026). Generated video cost ~$0.07-$6.00 per 30s depending on model; premium Veo-3 output ~$20-$30/min (shhots, ai-content.agency, 2026).
- Human UGC benchmark: average ~$198 per deliverable (Billo data via influee, 2025-26), beginners $150-$300/video, established $400-$800; usage rights add 30-50% (joinbrands, influee, 2026). AI-UGC explicitly cited as a reason human prices fell.
- Budgets: influencer marketing $32.55B global 2026; 87.49% of brands expect budget increases, 72.22% expect +50% or more; UGC creators the most expansionary segment (IMH 2026 via archive.com/sociallyin).

**Automation breakdown (est. 75%).**
- AI end-to-end: script and hook variants (Claude), avatar video (Creatify $39-$99 or Arcads), voice (ElevenLabs ~$5-$22/mo, not verified), captions and cutdowns (CapCut), file delivery and client folders (n8n/Make + Drive), invoicing (Stripe).
- Human required: intake call and angle selection, QA for uncanny or off-brand output, compliance (AI disclosure in ad settings, no unsubstantiated claims, no synthetic testimonials framed as real customers), sales outreach and portfolio.

**Economics.** Startup ~$80-$200 first month. Time to first dollar 1-4 weeks (Fiverr/Upwork plus cold DMs to Shopify stores with active TikTok ads). Realistic month-6: $300-$1,500/mo. Saturation: high at the $5-$35 Fiverr floor; medium for a performance-framed offer to brands already buying ads. Policy risk: medium (Meta and TikTok require AI disclosure for synthetic people in ads; TikTok bars virtual-influencer experience claims; some brand categories such as health need extra care).

**Verdict.** The strongest AI-native offer in this lens for someone with no audience. Cross-ref [[research/candidates/ai-ugc-ad-variant-batches]].

## Whop Content Rewards clipping (AI-clipped volume across 3-5 accounts)

**Offer.** Join paid clipping campaigns on Whop (and similar: Clipaffiliates, ClipMob, Sidekick), auto-clip long-form source into 20-40 captioned Shorts/TikToks/Reels per week, post across several accounts, submit for per-1,000-view payouts.

**Demand evidence.**
- Payout rates 2026: music/logo campaigns ~$1 per 1k views; standard $1-$3; UGC/talking-head $2-$50; live campaigns $0.50-$1.50; public listings cluster $0.20-$6 with an average near $1 (openclip, luvkaizen, vyroclips, 2026; anecdotal/marketplace observation).
- Scale: Forbes (April 2026, via clipaffiliates/nysos) reported Content Rewards paying out >$40,000/day across ~1M videos/month, clients including Polymarket, ElevenLabs, Justin Bieber; ~98,000 registered clippers; brands spending >$1M/month. Whop raised $200M at a $1.6B valuation in Feb 2026 (verified press via secondary).
- Distribution: independently tracked payouts of $2.58M across 8,466 earners, $887k in Feb 2026 alone (clipaffiliates, 2026). Arithmetic: ~$305 lifetime per earner; budgets are pooled per campaign and stop paying when exhausted; complaints about rejected views are common (findclout, 2026).

**Automation breakdown (est. 70%).**
- AI end-to-end: clip detection and virality scoring (OpusClip/Vizard/Klap ~$15-$30/mo, not verified), captions, reframing, hook text, multi-account scheduling, view tracking and submission logs (n8n + spreadsheet).
- Human required: picking the moment and writing the hook line, warming up accounts, choosing campaigns whose budgets are not already drained, disputing rejected views, staying inside each campaign's originality rules.

**Economics.** Startup ~$20-$60/mo. Time to first dollar 1-3 weeks (verification windows). Realistic month-6: $50-$500/mo; outliers higher. Saturation: high. Policy risk: high on distribution (YouTube inauthentic-content and Meta unoriginal-content policies target exactly this kind of reposted material; clipped accounts rarely qualify for platform monetization).

**Verdict.** Use for a few weeks to learn what hooks work and bank a few hundred dollars, then move the skill into candidate 4. Cross-ref [[research/candidates/whop-clipping-volume]].

## Direct clipping retainers for podcasters, coaches and webinar hosts

**Offer.** Done-for-you: 20-40 captioned vertical clips per month from a client's long-form (podcast, YouTube, webinars, course calls), scheduled to their TikTok/Reels/Shorts/LinkedIn with a monthly report; $400-$1,000/mo.

**Demand evidence.**
- Demand-side proxy: brands and creators are paying >$1M/month for clipping through Whop alone (Forbes April 2026 via secondary), and per-clip and per-view rates for hired clippers are a standard line item (nysos "How to hire clippers in 2026"; specific numbers not captured this session).
- Small-business social retainers: $500-$2,500/mo typical (WebFX, Sprout 2026); Clutch 2025 survey: 44% of small businesses spend $500-$2,000/mo on social management (via socialrails/thesearchsherpa, 2026).
- Podcast-specific clipping retainer pricing: no data found (search budget exhausted).

**Automation breakdown (est. 70%).**
- AI end-to-end: RSS/YouTube watcher pulls new episodes (n8n), OpusClip auto-clips and captions, Claude writes titles/hooks/descriptions in client voice, Metricool or Buffer schedules (~$0-$22/mo, not verified), monthly analytics report generated automatically.
- Human required: taste in selecting 20-40 of the ~100 candidate clips, brand-fit and sensitivity checks, client onboarding and monthly call, first-week style calibration.

**Economics.** Startup ~$40-$80/mo. Time to first dollar 2-6 weeks (offer one free week of clips to 10 mid-size podcasters). Realistic month-6: $400-$2,000/mo with 1-3 clients. Saturation: medium (many editors, but "clip + schedule + report" is less commoditized than per-clip gigs). Policy risk: low (client-owned original content).

**Verdict.** The best "AI does the editing, human does the taste" service; pairs naturally with LinkedIn ghostwriting for the same client. Cross-ref [[research/candidates/podcast-clipping-retainer]].

## Vertical-niche local business short-form package (restaurants, med-spas, home services)

**Offer.** 12-16 Reels/TikToks plus 4 carousels per month for one local vertical, $500-$1,000/mo; client sends phone footage weekly or the operator films a 2-hour batch monthly; includes captions, scheduling, and a one-page monthly report.

**Demand evidence.**
- Pricing: small businesses pay $500-$2,500/mo for social management (WebFX 2026); tiers reported as Basic $300-$500 (8-12 posts, 2-3 platforms), Standard $500-$1,000 (15-20 posts), Premium $1,000-$2,000 (Search Sherpa / Socialrails 2026, anecdotal agency ranges); freelancer hourly $35-$150 (Sprout 2026).
- Spend: Clutch 2025 survey, 44% of small businesses spend $500-$2,000/mo on social management (survey via secondary).
- Format performance: Reels reach rate 30.81%, 1.36x carousels, 2.1x more non-followers than other formats; single-image posts reach -21.96% YoY, engagement -45.98% (Metricool 2026 Instagram study and related 2026 studies via storrito/collabkit; survey/analytics).

**Automation breakdown (est. 58%).**
- AI end-to-end: monthly content calendar and captions/hooks/hashtags (Claude), auto-captioning and template edits (CapCut), carousel rendering from templates (Canva Bulk Create ~$15/mo, not verified), scheduling and reporting (Metricool), review-response drafts, prospect list building (Google Maps scrape via n8n).
- Human required: filming or collecting footage, owner relationship and approvals, on-site presence for launches/events, community replies needing judgment, sales.

**Economics.** Startup ~$50-$150. Time to first dollar 2-6 weeks (walk-ins, local Facebook groups, Chamber, a free 3-post trial). Realistic month-6: $1,000-$2,500/mo with 2-3 clients. Saturation: medium (every town has freelancers; a single vertical with a repeatable system and proof differentiates). Policy risk: low (original content).

**Verdict.** Most reliable path to $1k+/mo in this lens; least AI-leveraged of the top picks because of filming. Cross-ref [[research/candidates/local-vertical-shortform-package]].

## LinkedIn founder ghostwriting: "voice interview to 8-12 posts" pipeline

**Offer.** Weekly 30-minute recorded call with a founder/executive converted into 2-3 LinkedIn posts per week plus one carousel (PDF document) per month; $1,000-$2,000/mo entry retainer, moving to $2,500+ with proof.

**Demand evidence.**
- Rates 2026: most ghostwriters $1,000-$5,000/mo; budget writers from ~$500; premium $5,000-$15,000+ (mylance, windmillgrowth, foundera, 2026; anecdotal/agency ranges). Starter tier $200-$350/post or $1,200-$2,000/mo for 4-6 posts; mid-tier $350-$600/post or $2,500-$4,500/mo (getcreator 2026). Retainers dominate; annual contracts discounted 10-15%.
- Platform stance: LinkedIn's March 2026 "Authenticity Update" reportedly reduces reach of generic AI-written posts by up to 47% and LinkedIn is quoted as "correctly identifying generic content 94% of the time" in early testing (zoomsphere, futurefactors, teract, magicpost 2026; third-party, not verified against LinkedIn). Forbes (2026-07-23, Jodie Cook) lists five content moves LinkedIn started punishing in 2026. Mechanism reported as suppression of distribution beyond first-degree network, not removal.

**Automation breakdown (est. 60%).**
- AI end-to-end: transcription (Whisper/Descript ~$24/mo, not verified), style guide built from the client's past posts, Claude drafts in client voice with hook variants, carousel layout (Claude to Canva/PDF), scheduling (Taplio/Buffer), comment-opportunity list.
- Human required: running the interview and pulling out specific stories and opinions, editing every draft for concrete detail (the thing the algorithm now rewards), client approvals, engagement strategy, sales and case studies.

**Economics.** Startup ~$20-$60/mo. Time to first dollar 2-8 weeks (do 1-2 discounted case studies for proof; no audience needed if outreach targets seed-stage founders and agency owners). Realistic month-6: $1,000-$4,000/mo with 1-2 clients. Saturation: medium-high, but most competitors ship templated AI output, which is now penalized; that is the wedge. Policy risk: medium (generic-AI reach penalty; ghostwriting itself is permitted).

**Verdict.** Highest revenue per hour in this lens. Cross-ref [[research/candidates/linkedin-founder-ghostwriting]].

## Productized carousel design (Instagram + LinkedIn) for coaches and B2B

**Offer.** Turn a client's newsletter, podcast or long posts into 8-10 branded carousels per month, $300-$600/mo (or $40-$75 per carousel); sold standalone or as an add-on to the two retainer offers above.

**Demand evidence.**
- Carousels earn a 6.90% median engagement rate, roughly 109% more engagement per person reached than Reels; Reels reach 1.36x more (2026 studies via storrito, collabkit, blckalpaca; survey/analytics relayed by secondary sources).
- Metricool 2026 Instagram study: Reels and carousels are reshaping strategy; Instagram delivers 4.7x more views and 5.8x more interactions for carousels than TikTok; single images losing ground (reach -21.96% YoY).
- Marketplace price/count for carousel gigs: no data found (Fiverr blocked in this session). Nearest proxy: Basic social packages at $300-$500/mo for 8-12 posts.

**Automation breakdown (est. 75%).**
- AI end-to-end: Claude structures source content into slide-by-slide copy and hooks; Canva Bulk Create, Bannerbear or Placid via n8n renders templates; captions and alt text; delivery folders.
- Human required: one-time template design per client, QA, revisions, client feedback loop, sales.

**Economics.** Startup ~$15-$40/mo. Time to first dollar 1-4 weeks. Realistic month-6: $300-$1,200/mo. Saturation: high as a standalone commodity design gig; low policy risk (original content).

**Verdict.** Add-on, not a headline business. Cross-ref [[research/candidates/carousel-design-productized]].

## Faceless finance/tech Shorts channel with human-written scripts (funnel, flagged)

**Offer.** 5-7 Shorts per week in a high-RPM niche (personal finance explainers, consumer tech tips) with human-written scripts, AI voice, stock or AI b-roll, monetized through affiliate links, a newsletter or a TikTok Shop mirror; YPP AdSense as a bonus only.

**Demand evidence.**
- Shorts RPM ranges: $0.03-$0.10 (vidiq), $0.07-$0.20 (miraflow), $0.01-$0.07 (others); finance about 10x comedy/lifestyle RPM; US/UK/CA occasionally $0.15 for finance/tech; creators keep 45% of Shorts ad share (2026 articles; anecdotal/analytics mix). Arithmetic: 1M Shorts views/month is ~$50-$150.
- Policy: YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" in July 2025, broadening it to content lacking genuine human creativity; January 2026 enforcement wave reportedly terminated 16 channels with 35M combined subscribers and 4.7B views; 3-strike system (warning, 90-day suspension, permanent YPP removal); AI content remains eligible if it offers original value and the altered/synthetic disclosure is toggled (scalelab, flocker, arwriterai, vexub 2026; secondary reporting of YouTube policy).
- YPP threshold widely reported as 1,000 subscribers plus 10M public Shorts views in 90 days; not verified this session.

**Automation breakdown (est. 70%).**
- AI end-to-end: topic research, first-draft scripts (must be rewritten by a human), ElevenLabs voice, b-roll sourcing (Pexels free / Runway), CapCut assembly, n8n scheduling, title/description generation.
- Human required: script originality, opinion and fact-checking, hook selection, disclosure toggling, affiliate offer selection.

**Economics.** Startup ~$30-$100/mo. Time to first dollar 3-9+ months. Realistic month-6: $0-$200/mo from AdSense; affiliate upside undocumented. Saturation: high. Policy risk: high.

**Verdict.** Only run as a top-of-funnel for one of the service offers, never as the plan. Cross-ref [[research/candidates/faceless-finance-shorts-funnel]].

## Saturated or restricted in this lens

- **Repost meme/theme pages.** Meta's unoriginal-content crackdown took effect 2025-07-15: repeated reuse of others' videos/photos/text loses monetization access for a period and gets reduced distribution on everything; ~500,000 accounts actioned for spam/fake engagement and ~10M impersonation profiles removed in the first half of 2025 (TechCrunch 2025-07-14, Tubefilter 2025-07-15, Forbes 2025-07-15; verified press). Forum consensus in 2026 is "meme pages are dead," with only original-content niche pages still profitable (BlackHatWorld 2026; anecdotal). Shoutout rates for nano pages are $25-$150 per static post (influee 2026). Not recommended.
- **Pure-AI faceless Shorts farms** (template scripts, synthetic voice, volume uploads): the explicit target of YouTube's inauthentic-content enforcement and the January 2026 termination wave; Meta mirrors it. Only human-creative variants survive, and Shorts RPM makes AdSense immaterial anyway.
- **Whop clipping as a full-time plan**: pooled budgets, ~$300 lifetime average per earner, rejected-view complaints, and distribution policies that punish reposts.
- **AI voices or pre-recorded audio in TikTok Shop LIVEs**: banned since 2026-05-23; violations hit the Creator Health Rating that gates commissions (hyper-star, influencers-time 2026; secondary reporting of TikTok Shop policy).
- **Virtual-influencer product testimonials on TikTok Shop**: experience-based claims from synthetic presenters are disallowed; brands such as SharkNinja ban AI affiliate content outright and remove commissions (affiversemedia 2026).
- **Generic AI LinkedIn posting services**: March 2026 Authenticity Update suppresses templated AI output (up to 47% reach loss reported; third-party).
- **$5-$10 AI-UGC Fiverr gigs**: race to the bottom; compete on ad-testing outcomes instead.

# flow-lab index (map of content)

## Context refresh (read this first)

- **The question:** which three AI-leveraged side hustles should one person in the US run, with demand evidence and automation evidence for each, given under $500 of starting capital, 10-15 h/week, fluency with Claude/ChatGPT and n8n/Make, no audience, no credential, and a stated interest in YouTube and social media. Operator profile and selection criteria: [[research/shortlist]].
- **How it was answered:** 8 market lenses (62 candidates) -> skeptic sweep of saturated categories -> shortlist of 10 -> one dossier + two adversarial skeptic reviews (demand, execution) per candidate -> 3 judge rankings (speed, automation, durability) -> portfolio decision -> full report package (report, automation stack, 90-day plan) for each finalist. All 20 skeptic verdicts were "weakened"; the judge notes and report packages carry the corrected numbers.
- **Finalist 1, cash-flow engine: LinkedIn founder ghostwriting** from a weekly 30-minute voice interview (8-12 hand-edited posts + carousel/month for small B2B agency owners). Startup $55-75; tools $56-66/mo; first payment 4-12 weeks (median ~7) via a $300-600 prepaid pilot after ~200-300 hand-sent touches; retainer $500-1,200 then $800-1,500; month-6 base $1,600-2,400 on 12.5-13 h/wk (~$28-43/h pre-tax); automation 45-50% early, ~55% steady. If you can only do one, do this. Package: [[reports/linkedin-founder-ghostwriting/report]].
- **Finalist 2, durable YouTube retainer: fractional YouTube channel manager for RIAs, law firms and CPAs** (one recording/month -> 3 episodes, 8-10 Shorts, tested titles/thumbnails, compliance package, memo). Startup ~$150 budget ($59-124 cash); tools $112-156/mo (midpoint $125); first dollar 6-10 weeks via a $150-300 A/B pack, first retainer 2-4 months; Core $800/mo (band $600-1,000); month-6 base $1,600 (2 clients, range $1,000-2,000) at 10-13 h/wk (~$26-34/h); automation 25-40%, 16-22 h per client-month, two clients maximum. Package: [[reports/youtube-manager-expert-firms/report]].
- **Finalist 3, scalable MRR system (conditional): inbound AI front desk for 1-5-truck plumbing/HVAC/electrical shops** on Retell + Twilio + n8n, sold locally at $149-249/mo + $250-500 setup. Startup ~$150; fixed tools ~$50/mo + $30-45/shop; first dollar ~day 35-60 in the base ramp (60-90 if the first pilot slips); month-6 base $250-750 MRR (~3 shops), gross margin ~77-85% after payment fees; automation 45-55% overall (delivery 85-90% machine); 10-12 h/wk; ~$9-10/h at month 6. Conditional on the day job allowing 30+ local in-person/phone touches per week in 7-8 am, lunch and 4-6 pm windows; otherwise swap in podcast clipping. Package: [[reports/ai-front-desk-home-services/report]].
- **Sequencing:** ghostwriting outreach week 1; YouTube A/B packs from week 2-3; front-desk local outreach only after the first ghostwriting pilot is paid (~week 6-8). Total startup ~$355-375 plus ~$215-256/mo of tools at full load, inside the $500 cap only if staged. Each finalist has a 60-day kill criterion (details in [[decisions/final-selection]]).
- **First alternate: podcast-to-Shorts clipping retainer** (speed #2, automation #3, durability #5; corrected month-6 base $500-1,200). Lost the slot because it overlaps the YouTube manager's stack and buyer and is the fastest-commoditizing service (TikTok Smart Split, YouTube Studio highlights, Riverside Magic Clips free). Replaces the front desk if local outreach is impossible, or ghostwriting if 150 messages produce zero pilots.
- **Other runners-up and why they lost:** hyperlocal newsletter + AI audio (durability #2 but needs the operator living in the county doing 4-6 h/wk of face-to-face ad sales; month-6 $200-800); AI-actor ad packs for DTC (TikTok Symphony and Meta Advantage+ give the variants away; $230-335/mo tool floor; NY synthetic-performer law); B2B lead lists (Apollo's Feb 2026 Terms block delivering exports to clients; California Delete Act; $4-9/h); n8n workflow tutorial channel (n8n Assistant builds workflows natively; first cash 3-5 months; $60-250 at month 6); personalized AI kids books (free Gemini Storybook, Etsy API has no messaging, COPPA; $2-4/h); TikTok Shop affiliate (needs 1,000-5,000 followers first; ~$45/mo mean commission for non-elite affiliates; $1-5/h).
- **Cross-cutting risk 1, platform AI-content policy:** YouTube's inauthentic-content rule (Jul 2025, clarified 16 Jul 2026) demonetizes templated AI channels and "AI personas presenting as experts on health, legal, finance"; LinkedIn's "Seems like AI slop" button (2026-07-30, 1M+ reports, ~40% fewer views on classified slop, poster notified); Meta's unoriginal-content crackdown; TikTok AI-voice bans in Shop LIVE. Every survivor sells human judgment with AI underneath and never ships unedited AI output.
- **Cross-cutting risk 2, marketplace and vendor AI rules:** Etsy requires AI disclosure and bans browser automation; Fiverr requires disclosure and rejects unmodified AI output; KDP requires AI disclosure; Apollo restricts self-serve data to internal use; LinkedIn's User Agreement 8.2 bars bots and automated messaging (hand-send everything); CapCut's ToS grants ByteDance a licence over uploads.
- **Cross-cutting risk 3, price compression on Fiverr/Upwork:** Upwork Q2 2026 GSV -4% and Fiverr marketplace revenue -15.5% as low-complexity work is automated; commodity writing, clipping, thumbnails and lead lists clear at $5-40 per job; the "$30 middle market has collapsed". Sell retainers off-marketplace, never per-unit AI output.
- **Cross-cutting risk 4, hidden manual work:** every dossier's automation share fell 10-30 points under execution review (sales and onboarding, transcript and clip QA, platform babysitting, 10DLC registration, compliance review, revision rounds, credit metering). No candidate exceeds ~55% time-weighted automation; nothing here is passive at 10-15 h/wk.
- **Legal items that recur:** recording consent for AI notetakers and voice agents (11-15 all-party states; Otter and Fireflies litigation), FTC fake-testimonial rule ($53,088/violation), TCPA for any outbound AI voice or SMS (keep the front desk inbound-only), SEC Marketing Rule / FINRA 2210 / state-bar ad filing fees for the YouTube manager, and uncopyrightable pure-AI output (human editing is the authorship step).
- **Evidence caveat for everything in this vault:** no session could fetch a primary page (egress proxy); every figure is a search-snippet, GitHub-mirror or operator-log relay, dated where possible. Each report has a "verify first" list; treat the picks as "start here and validate inside 60 days", not forecasts.
- **Where detail lives:** finalist economics, risks and kill criteria in `reports/<slug>/report.md`; workflows, tool tiers and copy-paste prompts in `automation-stack.md`; week-1, 30/60/90-day gates and templates in `plan.md`; why these three in [[decisions/final-selection]]; all ten candidates side by side in [[decisions/comparison-matrix]]; per-lens rankings in the three judge notes; raw evidence and skeptic corrections in `research/candidates/`; market surveys in `research/lenses/`; what not to do in [[research/saturated-overhyped]].

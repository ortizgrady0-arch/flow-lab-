## TL;DR

- One row per candidate dossier under `research/candidates/`, with the dossier's own key numbers, both skeptic verdicts, each judge's rank (score in brackets) and the finalist flag from [[decisions/final-selection]].
- Finalists: [[research/candidates/linkedin-founder-ghostwriting]], [[research/candidates/youtube-manager-expert-firms]], [[research/candidates/ai-front-desk-home-services]] (conditional). First alternate: [[research/candidates/podcast-clipping-retainer]].
- Every dossier self-score was "weakened" by both its demand and execution skeptic; no candidate was refuted outright and none survived with a 30-day first dollar.
- Finalist rows carry the **report-corrected** numbers from `reports/<slug>/report.md` (revised after review); non-finalist rows carry the pre-review dossier numbers, which the skeptic notes and judge notes cut by roughly 30-60% on revenue.
- Judge rank-sums: front desk 9, ghostwriting 10, clipping 10, YouTube manager 12, then a gap to 16 (hyperlocal newsletter, ad packs); the top four were treated as a statistical tie and the portfolio rule decided the third slot.
- Demand score is the dossier's own 1-10; the corrected view per candidate lives in the two skeptic notes and in the three judge notes ([[decisions/judge-speed-and-feasibility]], [[decisions/judge-automation-leverage]], [[decisions/judge-durability-and-risk]]).

## Reading guide

- **Every self-score was weakened.** Each dossier was audited by a demand skeptic and an execution skeptic, and all twenty verdicts came back "weakened" (a handful of individual claims were refuted, e.g. Apollo's Terms for the lead-list stack, the "beehiiv free tier" for the newsletter, the 47% LinkedIn reach figure). Treat the demand score and the non-finalist key numbers above as the optimistic bound, not the estimate.
- **The judge notes carry the corrected view.** The three judges re-scored every candidate on skeptic-corrected numbers; their ranks in this table, not the dossier scores, are what [[decisions/final-selection]] used. Typical corrections: month-6 base revenue cut 30-60%, automation share cut 10-30 points, first dollar pushed from ~30 days to 6-12 weeks.
- **Finalist rows are report-corrected.** The three report packages ([[reports/linkedin-founder-ghostwriting/report]], [[reports/youtube-manager-expert-firms/report]], [[reports/ai-front-desk-home-services/report]]) were revised after review and are authoritative for those rows; the pre-review dossier numbers for the finalists were: ghostwriting 58% / $50 / $45 / 35 d / $2,500 / 7 h; YouTube manager 58% / $150 / $110 / 30 d / $3,000 / 12 h; front desk 65% / $150 / $50 / 40 d / $1,650 / 12 h.
- **Non-finalist corrected numbers, for quick reference (from the skeptic and judge notes):** clipping month-6 base $500-1,200, tools $120-180 at 3 clients; lead lists $400-600 at $4-9/h, Apollo seat must be the client's; hyperlocal newsletter $200-800, tools $75-125, 14-18 h/wk on site; ad packs $700-1,000, tools $230-335; tutorial channel $60-250, first cash 3-5 months; kids books ~$250-500 gross at $2-4/h; TikTok Shop $50-300 at $1-5/h, first deposit 75-150 days.
- **Evidence caveat inherited from every note:** no session fetched a primary page; all figures are search-snippet, GitHub-mirror or operator-log relays. Verify pricing and policy pages before spending money.

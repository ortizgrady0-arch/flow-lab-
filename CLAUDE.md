# flow-lab — project brain

This repo holds an Obsidian vault at `brain/` that is the project's memory.

## Context-refresh rule (saves tokens)
1. Read `brain/00-Index.md` first. Its "Context refresh" section summarizes the whole project.
2. Open other notes only when you need detail, and read just their `## TL;DR` block unless the task needs more.
3. When you finish work, update the relevant note's TL;DR and the index instead of re-explaining in chat.

## Vault layout
- `brain/00-Index.md` — map of content, start here
- `brain/reports/<hustle>/` — report.md, automation-stack.md, plan.md for each chosen side hustle
- `brain/decisions/` — final selection, judge notes, comparison matrix
- `brain/research/` — shortlist, candidate dossiers with skeptic notes, lens research, saturated/overhyped hustles

## Conventions
- Every note: YAML frontmatter (title, tags, updated), `## TL;DR` first (max 8 bullets), `[[wikilinks]]` by vault-relative path, `## Sources` last.
